## Investigated and mitigated Hermes Miller CPU loop

- **WHAT:** Found PID `87268` (`miller serve`, parent Hermes gateway) burning a core while rooted at `/Users/murphy/.hermes`. Updated the machine-local `/Users/murphy/.hermes/.julieignore` to exclude volatile/generated state (`.miller/`, `logs/`, `cron/`, `sessions/`, `memories/`) and patched Miller's generated `.julieignore` baseline to include `.miller/`.
- **WHY:** The live artifact was repeatedly recording revisions for `.miller/logs/miller-20260618.jsonl` plus Hermes cron/session state even with no MCP tool calls beyond `tools/list`. That self-indexed Miller's own generated sidecar and forced repeated freshness swaps plus sidecar convergence.
- **HOW:** Process/log/artifact evidence showed `/Users/murphy/.hermes/.miller/symbols.db` revisions climbing from ~118 to 194 via `scan/incremental`; `revision_file_changes` was dominated by `.miller/logs/miller-20260618.jsonl`. After adding the local ignores, revision 193 pruned `.miller` and cron output from the artifact, and CPU dropped from a hot core to under 1%.
- **IMPACT:** The active Hermes Miller loop stopped on this machine, and new Miller-generated `.julieignore` files will exclude `.miller/` so fresh workspaces do not self-index the Miller sidecar.
