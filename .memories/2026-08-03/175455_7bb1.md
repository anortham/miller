## #18 CLOSED: experiment ladder proves a validated 4× cold-scan win

**WHAT:** Ran the two-step experiment ladder on dotnet/runtime (same box/argv as the morning baseline) and extended `docs/findings/2026-08-03-dotnet-runtime-v2231-baseline.md` with the results.

**Ladder:** baseline 76.3 min → Exp 1 (8 GiB bulk cache via `JULIE_BULK_CACHE_KIB`) 47.0 min → Exp 2 (+ `JULIE_RESOLUTION_NO_SAVEPOINT`) **18.8 min = 4.05×**. All three artifacts byte-size-identical AND logically verified identical (`identifier_resolutions` outcome/tier/method/confidence distributions, aggregates, pending counts).

**HOW/WHY (root cause chain):**
- 128 MiB `SQLITE_BULK_CACHE_SIZE_KIB` vs 12+ GiB working set → pread thrash then mid-savepoint dirty spill (sys CPU 676→105 s once fixed).
- Cache fix unmasked `memjrnlTruncate` at ~82% of resolution samples: 12.86 M identifiers ÷ FLUSH_CHUNK 500 ≈ 30 k statement-ends each walking the whole-pass `SAVEPOINT resolution_hook` multi-GiB memjrnl — the v2.9.0 quadratic reborn at scale. Removing the savepoint cut ~1,330 s of pure CPU; profile now shows real resolver work (tier_candidates, memcmp, #17 BTreeMap).

**Production fix shape (julie-extractors, next minor):** (1) machine/artifact-aware bulk cache sizing (flat 8 GiB hostile to 16 GiB boxes — peak RSS hit 30.1 GiB); (2) resolution in its OWN transaction post scan-commit — sound because failure semantics are already "rows stay unresolved". Experiment knobs committed on `resolver-decay` branch: `d940e0c`, `7ffaa3c` (defaults unchanged, skip mode measurement-only/unsound rollback).

**IMPACT:** #18 investigation closed; #15 unblocked; levels program economics improve 4× for background convergence but 18.8 min cold still justifies levels (L-P3 yardstick updated). Next: user discussion on levels P0 design + sequencing the julie-extractors 2.24.0 fix release.
