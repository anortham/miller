## WHAT

Reviewed the idle-quiet freshness-stamp and WAL-checkpoint change that landed as `b6b49d1f` and was merged into `main` as `32b3bd53`. No source files were edited.

## WHY

The change is intended to stop 500 ms idle opens of `store.db` while preserving freshness correctness and to truncate family-store WALs after writes without delaying extraction.

## HOW

- Traced the stamp trust protocol from coordinator mutation through `WorkspaceReadSessionFactory.Probe`.
- Audited all `IJulieStoreClient.Submit` and `StoreWorkspaceCoordinator.Scan/Update/Delete` production call paths.
- Checked `JulieStoreClient` mutation-anchor lifetime against the debounce maintenance timing.
- Compared per-view stamp invalidation with `StoreLogCursor`, whose cursor includes global and shared-version events.
- Ran the new focused stamp/checkpoint tests in an isolated artifacts directory: 6/6 passed.

## IMPACT

The review found release-blocking correctness gaps: delete/replace failures can leave a trusted stale stamp; sibling-view writes can advance another view's cursor without invalidating its stamp; catch-up can race a sibling writer; and WAL maintenance is armed only for debounce-drain writes and can race a subsequent extract after `_opsGate` release. Mismatched stamps are not healed, and permanent SQLite errors are retried as Busy every empty tick. These gaps mean the merged change does not yet meet the stated correctness or idle-quiet goal.
