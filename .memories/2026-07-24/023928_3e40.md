Implemented the first Phase 6 TDD slice for risk-ranked impact.

## WHY

- Extended evidence-bearing reach results with predecessor, edge kind/confidence/source, centrality, and visibility.

- Added a pure impact peer ranker with hop primary.
- Added visibility to indexed symbols and graph nodes.

- Added per-row compact and JSON impact explanations.
- Filtered file seeds to actionable symbol kinds.
