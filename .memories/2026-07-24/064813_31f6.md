## WHAT

Closed the Phase 10 product-attribution gap with `takeover-product-verdict-v1`.

- Added a strict privacy-safe JSON schema and froze its hash into preflight identity.
- Added `attest-product` for operator-side emission and `validate-safe-return` for fail-closed receipt validation.
- Revalidates the canonical full 30-task, 13-capability decision aggregate, exact file hash, zero voids, and both pass verdicts.
- Updated the evaluator contract, benchmark README, and sealed protocol.

## WHY

The neutral safe aggregate intentionally hides product mapping and could not prove the passing verdict applied to Miller. A hand-authored statement was too error-prone and unenforced.

## HOW

The operator applies the frozen private mapping and emits only a strict attestation bound to the exact safe aggregate bytes. The receiving session never receives the mapping artifact. Documentation now honestly notes that verdict correlation can make the role inferable after the run while all row-level evidence and the mapping artifact remain private.

## IMPACT

Phase 10 now has an executable, schema-validated product attribution gate that fails closed on subset, incomplete, tampered, hash-mismatched, neutral-fail, or Miller-fail returns.
