## WHAT

Completed Phase 4 of the Miller Julie-takeover remediation: shared deterministic symbol reranking, AND-first relaxation, container evidence, typed mixed file/symbol routing, per-call retrieval control, and explicit exact-identifier no-hit outcomes.

## WHY

Miller needed fewer search calls, higher top-result relevance, honest lexical/semantic policy control, and one-call proof for absent exact symbols without expanding the MCP surface.

## HOW

- Added pure transparent reranking with raw BM25 retained separately from exactness, phrase proximity, source/path/language, and container contributions.
- Added strict AND then bounded stable OR fallback across FTS5 and in-memory backends.
- Added parent container evidence from extractor `parent_id` relationships and automatic semantic abstention only when that evidence wins rank one.
- Added typed mixed auto routing and `retrieval=auto|lexical|hybrid|semantic`; lexical performs zero vector work and forced arms fail loudly.
- Added exact-miss typing for identifier-shaped fuzzy matches and removed a stale active-guidance mention of the hard-removed `trace auto` mode.
- Claude review found forced hybrid could silently serve lexical output when an injected arm declined; added observed served-result enforcement and a regression test. Three other review suggestions were rejected after validating intended contracts.

## IMPACT

Visible takeover calibration now ranks all four positive Goldfish/Eros queries first and resolves the true-no-hit task in one call. Both lexical backends share rank-identical behavior, explicit retrieval modes are testable, and semantic-off/lexical paths preserve zero vector work.
