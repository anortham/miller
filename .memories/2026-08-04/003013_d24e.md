## Fixed Windows CI broker teardown 1s stall (windows-fast twice-failed test)

`PassiveObservation_HealthSilenceRespectsTheTotalWallClockBound` failed on both Windows CI runs at ~1.0s (0.9996s, 1.0116s) against a 500ms bound — systematic, not flake.

- **Root cause:** `SemanticEmbeddingSession.CloseConnectionAsync` disposed the `SidecarLineReader` (cancel + `_pump.Wait(1s)` join) BEFORE closing the connection. The pump's in-flight named-pipe read on Windows can outlive cancellation, so nothing unblocked it and the join paid its full 1s cap. On Unix the cancellation lands, which is why only Windows failed.
- **Fix:** reorder — close the transport first (stream close deterministically unblocks the pump read on every platform: BrokerConnection closes the pipe/socket; ProcessConnection kills the child, closing its stdout), then join the pump. Also widened the test bound 500ms → 2s: still proves the invariant (the stub's 5s health silence never propagates to the caller) without a brittle wall-clock assumption on loaded CI VMs.
- **Impact:** removes a real bounded ~1s stall on every Windows semantic-session teardown against a silent peer (e.g. `workspace health` passive observation of a stalled broker). Fast suite green locally: 5,842 passed, 0 failed.
