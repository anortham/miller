- Preserved existing warnings, recovery behavior, full/incremental selection, and `IndexerServiceScanTests` coverage.

### IMPACT

- The remaining indexer orchestration work can target registry publishing, leadership/yield handling, watcher topology, or test hooks without reworking sidecar policy again.
