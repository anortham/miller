## Audited Miller tool output efficiency

Completed a repo-local deep dive on Miller tool output shape, balancing compactness against enough context for the next agent step.

- **WHAT:** Added `CandidateOutput` and wired compact ambiguity rendering through it for `inspect`, `trace`, and `impact`; added a durable findings note at `docs/findings/2026-06-14-tool-output-efficiency-audit.md`.
- **WHY:** `inspect Search` produced a 10.7 KB / 139-line ambiguity response, and scoped same-file ambiguity still told agents to pass `scope=<file>`, causing unnecessary follow-up friction.
- **HOW:** Compact candidate output now caps at 20 entries, appends a remainder note, and chooses scope guidance only when candidates span multiple files. `InspectTool` also normalizes multiline signatures into one compact row before truncation.
- **IMPACT:** `inspect Search` now measures about 2.6 KB / 22 lines, and same-file scoped ambiguity says to pass a more specific target. JSON/export contracts were left complete by design.
