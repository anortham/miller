## Continued IndexerService architecture split

### WHAT
- Added `WorkspaceRegistryScanPublisher` to centralize scan-stamp publishing for current-workspace registry rows.
- Wired `IndexerService` startup scan stamping through the required `MarkScanned` path and convergence stamping through best-effort `TryMarkScanned`.
- Added `IndexerWatcherSet` and `IndexerWatcherCallbacks` to own `FileSystemWatcher` construction, `.git/HEAD` watcher setup, ancestor `.gitignore` watcher setup, event subscription, and disposal.
- Replaced the old watcher fields/list in `IndexerService` with one `IndexerWatcherSet?` field while keeping event-routing handlers in `IndexerService`.
- Updated `TODO.md` with the registry and watcher lifecycle slices.

### WHY
The architecture-quality TODO called out `IndexerService` mixing registry marking and watcher topology with leadership/session logic. These were contained policy/lifecycle blocks that could be extracted without changing leadership or event-routing behavior.

### HOW
- Used TDD for both slices: added missing-type tests first for `WorkspaceRegistryScanPublisher` and `IndexerWatcherSet`.
- Preserved startup registry failure semantics by keeping `MarkScanned` throwing for required startup stamps.
- Preserved convergence behavior with a best-effort `TryMarkScanned` method that logs and returns false.
- Preserved watcher event routing by moving only watcher construction/subscription/disposal, not `HandleChanged`/rename/filter logic.

### IMPACT
`IndexerService` now delegates sidecar convergence, registry scan stamping, and watcher lifecycle setup/disposal. Remaining split candidates are the leadership/yield orchestration block and the broad test-hook surface.
