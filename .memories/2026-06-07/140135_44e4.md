## Implemented incremental search sidecar freshness

- **WHAT:** Replaced the fragile search sidecar rowid identity contract with explicit `doc_id`, added incremental `search.db` path updates from `revision_file_changes`, made enabled sidecar reads fail visibly when missing/stale/corrupt, and added sidecar status to workspace status output.
- **WHY:** The prior design only worked for full append-only sidecar rebuilds. Single-file watcher/write-through updates advanced `symbols.db` without advancing `search.db`, and the hidden in-memory fallback could mask stale/corrupt sidecars or allocate unexpectedly on large repositories.
- **HOW:** `SearchIndexWriter` now supports `ApplyFileChanges`; `SymbolSearchSidecar.EnsureCurrent` chooses full repair vs incremental delta; `IndexerService` and `CrossWorkspaceRefreshService` call convergence after scans and single-file updates; `WorkspaceIndexProvider` uses `OpenRequired` when the sidecar is enabled; `WorkspaceRender`/CLI/tool status show `search_db` state.
- **IMPACT:** Search sidecar freshness is now driven by the same incremental revision delta as the file watcher, stale sidecars are visible, and opting out with `MILLER_SEARCH_SIDECAR=0` remains the intentional in-memory path.
