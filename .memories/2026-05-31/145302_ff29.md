## Miller Task 5 cross-workspace provider and refresh service

- **WHAT:** Added the Task 5 `Miller.Server.Workspaces` seam: fixed `WorkspaceReadContext`, `WorkspaceRefreshResult`, `IWorkspaceIndexProvider`, `WorkspaceIndexProvider`, and `CrossWorkspaceRefreshService`; registered the registry, refresh service, and provider in `MillerServiceRegistration`; added provider and refresh-service tests.
- **WHY:** Read tools need one future routing seam that can serve the current workspace through a captured index snapshot or a registered workspace through the central registry, with refresh-first behavior when requested.
- **HOW:** Current workspace resolution captures `IndexHolder.Snapshot()` once and builds a fixed `SmartTargetResolver(index)`. Registered workspace resolution validates root safety, optionally refreshes, loads via `RepositoryIndexLoader.Load`, and caches by `(workspace_id, index_db_path, last_revision)`. Refresh acquires the target `.miller/indexer.lock`, calls Julie scan with `force:false`, marks registry state, and handles busy locks by polling for revision changes without scanning.
- **IMPACT:** Task 7 can route read tools through `IWorkspaceIndexProvider` without touching freshness or registry details. Tests cover snapshot consistency under holder swap, cache invalidation, refresh-before-load, lock-busy no-scan behavior, missing roots, sensitive roots, and missing DB scan creation.
