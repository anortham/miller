## Corrected Codex SessionStart hook diagnosis and history

- **IMPACT:** Codex 0.153.2 did not introduce this failure. Goldfish made its raw-output assumption in commit `2121441e` on 2026-07-16. Razorback's fallback dates to `368df7b` on 2026-04-08, but its plugin path remains correct.
