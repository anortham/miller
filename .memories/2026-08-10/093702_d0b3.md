## Restored progress-aware liveness for store artifact migration

Claude's adversarial review confirmed that `--from-artifact` was using a hard-cap-only wait policy even though Miller already sampled coordinator, generation-store, and process-output activity.

- **Decision:** use the normal progress-aware stall policy for all store requests, including artifact migration; retain the generous absolute hard cap as a runaway backstop.
- **Why:** Julie's `--from-artifact` mode rejects scan-only supervision flags, but its chunked durable work still changes the coordinator and generation databases; discarding those signals made a wedged migration wait silently for hours.
- **Verified:** focused policy tests 36/36, fast suite 6273 passed and 2 skipped, relevant live store tests 18/18, full local Scale suite 134 passed and 5 skipped against julie-extract 2.31.2.
- **Docs:** v1.18.0 release notes now describe the actual migration liveness behavior; CI timing remains report-only and local.
- **Open review disposition:** Claude's low-confidence UUID case is not present in the producer contract: Julie accepts lowercase UUIDs only and Miller passes `Guid.ToString("D")`.
