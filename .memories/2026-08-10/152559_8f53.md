## Closed two store recovery defects from the final paired review

Grok's final review of ad44128a found two confirmed high-severity defects; both reproduced with red tests.

- **WHAT:** Store-family resolution now fails closed when published `gen-*/store.db` evidence exists without `CURRENT`, instead of returning Planned and reimporting. Cross-workspace source reconciliation now removes the pointer and pending/recovery markers under the held writer lease, and reports incomplete marker cleanup as failed.
- **WHY:** A valid pointer must never enter a permissive stale-artifact import path, and cross-workspace cleanup must match bootstrap rollback cleanup.
- **HOW:** Added `HasPublishedGeneration` detection in `StoreFamilyResolver`; routed production refresh cleanup through `DeletePointerAfterSourceRebuild` while preserving injected test cleanup behavior. Added resolver and refresh regressions plus v4.12 A44/A45 contract/release-note amendments.
- **IMPACT:** The two new regressions pass; affected classes pass 69/69; full fast passes 6,306/6,308 with 2 skips; full scale passes 135/140 with 5 skips; Release build is 0 warnings/0 errors. Claude's medium malformed-pointer synchronous-recovery concern remains an intentional A5 liveness tradeoff, not a correctness failure.
