## WHAT

Diagnosed the Miller family-store convergence delay in `julie-extract store resolve`.

## WHY

A 98-file Miller-repo delta took 6m20s to become exact, far beyond the producer's ~10s Miller-scale performance gate.

## HOW

- Live store contains 415,975 identifier resolutions, 11,434 pending resolutions, and 56,433 distinct resolved target pairs.
- `StoreScratchResolutionSession.finish_exact` streams the full exact result, then `ResolutionBaseWriter.finish_with_target_lookup` validates every distinct target pair.
- The validation callback calls `StoreConnectionFactory.open_reader()` for each pair. Every open reopens the ~1 GB `store.db`, checks `user_version`, reads identity/version-floor metadata, sets and verifies pragmas, then performs one indexed existence query.
- That creates at least 56,433 SQLite connection opens for this corpus. The observed process had already performed 45.9 GB of logical reads after 4m37s, matching connection/schema-read amplification.
- The production resolver also always runs a forced full-corpus exact pass and then diffs it against the selected base, even when only 98 files changed.
- The synthetic performance fixture puts ~392k identifiers in one source version targeting essentially one symbol, so target-validation cardinality is near one and the N+1 connection-open cost is absent. Its recorded fresh time is ~10.2s versus ~380s live.

## IMPACT

The new storage format is functionally correct and read performance is fast, but convergence performance is not acceptable. The immediate producer fix is to reuse one validated reader (or batch/join target validation) across all target checks and add a high-cardinality target fixture. Incremental/row-scoped resolution and Miller cursor-incremental sidecars remain separate follow-up optimizations.
