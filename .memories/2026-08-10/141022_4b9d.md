## Final pre-commit store rollback race repair

A final code inspection found one remaining race after the prior malformed-pointer fix: bootstrap released the revalidation lock before the legacy binding decision completed.

- **WHAT:** Bootstrap now holds the workspace writer lease from malformed-pointer revalidation through the legacy binding return. The rollback helper accepts a caller-held lease and only owns a lease when called independently. A test proves a caller-held lease remains held after cleanup.
- **WHY:** A store-mode writer could otherwise install a valid pointer after revalidation but before legacy binding, recreating the exact mixed-mode race the prior fix intended to close.
- **HOW:** Reused the existing `SingleWriterLock` and `heldWriterLease` pattern; no new surface or protocol was added.
- **IMPACT:** The reviewed candidate now protects the complete cleanup-to-bind interval. This is the last confirmed correctness fix before the final Claude/Grok pair.
