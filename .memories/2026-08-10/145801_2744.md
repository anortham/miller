## Fixed bootstrap rollback retry classification

Claude's final paired review found that the store-off bootstrap revalidation call could throw an operational rollback error outside the retry wrapper, leaving the workspace permanently failed. Grok found no defect.

- **WHAT:** Added `StoreRollbackExporter.ExportForBootstrap`, routed both bootstrap rollback attempts through it, and added a regression for an unopenable store pointer.
- **WHY:** Every operational rollback failure must schedule the existing retry path rather than strand the workspace.
- **HOW:** The wrapper preserves all existing `ExportIfRequired` behavior and translates only `IsOperationalFailure` exceptions to `StoreRollbackRetryException`.
- **IMPACT:** Focused exporter/bootstrap coverage passes 29/29; local fast passes 6,304/6,306 with 2 skips; local scale passes 135/140 with 5 skips. CI timing remains report-only.

The candidate needs one fresh Claude/Grok pair after this fix. A7/A8 remain open Ph4/Ph5 gates.
