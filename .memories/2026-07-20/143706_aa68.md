## WHAT

Captured the deferred machine-service architecture in `docs/plans/2026-07-20-miller-machine-service-design.md` and saved the inactive brief `miller-machine-service-architecture-deferred`.

## WHY

Miller's registry, telemetry, dashboard, cross-workspace access, and semantic runtime increasingly need one truthful machine-wide owner, but implementation must not disrupt the semantic program currently in progress or recreate Julie's daemon update failures.

## HOW

The design keeps `miller` as the Native AOT public client, proposes a packaged non-AOT demand-started service, keeps artifacts per workspace, activates only used or pinned workspaces, shares one semantic session, and uses immutable side-by-side packages with a kernel-authoritative singleton and graceful upward handoff.

## IMPACT

The direction and acceptance gates are durable for later review. No semantic code or current semantic design was changed. The exact shared-service meaning of `MILLER_SEMANTIC=off`, protocol compatibility, pinning controls, and permanent direct mode remain explicit decisions before implementation planning.
