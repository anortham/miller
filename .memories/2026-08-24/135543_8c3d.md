## WHAT

Completed the Miller QML and Qt Quick Test audit synthesis.

## WHY

Generic artifact ingestion is not enough to claim first-class QML or continuous testing.

## HOW

- Generic extension discovery, indexing, search, inspect, trace, edit, and patterns can carry QML facts.
- No Miller QML fixture/tool regression exists; QML imports/qmldir have no query-time resolution policy; QML properties are excluded from semantic cards.
- CT inventory/provider factory cannot discover or run QML.
- Official Qt/CMake docs support a CMake/CTest-backed lane: QuickTest harness, tst_*.qml scanning, Test_Name::function selectors, offscreen mode, JSON CTest discovery, and JUnit XML.
- Native QML coverage requires separate Coco/CocoQML tooling, so initial support must declare coverage unavailable.

## IMPACT

Recommended program has three approval-gated slices: extractor/module quality, Miller QML evidence/resolution, and a cross-platform Qt Quick Test provider.
