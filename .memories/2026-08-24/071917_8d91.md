## WHAT

Implemented Task 2 of the performance-audit closure: cheap CT freshness probing, durable per-workspace cursor checkpoints, restart convergence, and bounded probe-to-impact drift retries.

## WHY

The 250 ms CT loop was opening a full Miller read session on every poll, and the in-memory cursor was lost on daemon restart. That caused compatibility projection work on unchanged ticks and could absorb an interval or arm from the live cursor without reconciling prior changes.

## HOW

- Extended `WorkspaceFreshnessProbe` with the exact stable `ctgen1:*` generation identity used by `CtIndexCursor`; family, legacy, and freshness-stamp probe paths populate it.
- Switched `MillerArtifactRevisionSource` from `WorkspaceReadSessionFactory.Open` to `Probe`.
- Added `ct_revision_cursors` (schema 3) and `ContinuousTestStore` read/write methods.
- Loaded the durable cursor once per workspace; startup reconciles it even before enqueue arming, saves only complete changed/empty intervals, and preserves it on rebuild/unavailable/malformed paths.
- Added three-total-attempt moving-cursor retry in `MillerFactImpactSource`, with internal session injection only for deterministic tests.
- Wired the store into primary and adopted-worktree pollers.

## IMPACT

Unchanged polls use the no-projection freshness path. Restarts replay a complete persisted interval instead of losing it, and moving generations fail closed without advancing durable freshness. Focused owned tests passed: 91/91; the final cursor/schema/poller subset passed 23/23. Indexing, Testing, and Server Debug builds passed with 0 warnings/errors. Task 7/8 edits remain unowned and unstaged in the shared worktree for the lead to reconcile.
