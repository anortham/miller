## WHAT

Completed and lead-reviewed Task 1 of the CT performance hardening plan. `JunitTestArtifactImporter` now resolves JUnit rows and validates selected-test attribution before writing artifacts, cases, runs, or results. `ContinuousTestCoordinator` supplies the selected ids and logs bounded partial-residue counts.

## WHY

Whole-suite xUnit transport is moving away from stdout JSON to JUnit. That makes artifact attribution the state-integrity boundary: an unrelated artifact must not mutate CT state, while valid whole-suite runs must still discover brand-new tests.

## HOW

A non-empty selected set with zero mapped rows fails mutation-atomically. Partial matches import mapped and new rows, keep unreported selected rows stale through the existing run completion semantics, and emit one ratio-only lifecycle diagnostic. Offline imports without selected ids preserve their existing behavior. The lead inspected the changed symbols and references with Miller and reviewed the complete owned diff against the plan.

## IMPACT

The importer/coordinator seam is ready for artifact-only xUnit whole-suite results. Focused worker evidence passed: importer 18/18, store applier 12/12, lifecycle log 7/7, plus `git diff --check`.
