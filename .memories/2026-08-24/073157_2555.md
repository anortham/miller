## WHAT

Final Task 2 review-fix state: poller-level re-probe convergence is implemented, cursor store helpers are internal, and the forward-drift regression uses the real Miller probe/session/impact path.

## WHY

The review required proof that a session can observe a newer revision than the preceding probe and that the same poll re-probes and completes the forward interval. The earlier test used a scripted impact result and was not strong enough.

## HOW

- `PollAsync` retries at most three total impact attempts. Every `moving_cursor` result triggers a fresh `RefreshAsync`; the new observation becomes the next `current` key. Identity changes return rebuild; missing/degraded observations stay fail-closed.
- `MillerFactImpactSource` has one full session read per call and returns `moving_cursor` on session/probe drift; it no longer retries stale `current` values.
- The real forward-drift test mutates the fixture after the first probe: the first full session sees revision 2 while the poll observed revision 1, the retry probes revision 2, and the second real impact read completes/enqueues revision 2.
- Cursor helpers are internal; Miller trace found no cross-assembly public caller.

## IMPACT

The same PollAsync converges a real moving cursor without retaining a session or overwriting durable state with an observed-but-unreconciled cursor. The old stale-retry behavior is removed. Task 7 provider files remain dirty and unstaged.
