## Approved CT performance design written; external-review spend boundary reached

### WHAT

Added the approved two-slice design covering the xUnit 48.5 MB result-transport regression and the daemon 250 ms all-state materialization.

### WHY

Both fixes are correctness-sensitive fail-safe paths. The design keeps public contracts unchanged, reuses the existing JUnit artifact import seam, and moves daemon verdict inputs to one indexed aggregate query.

### HOW

Miller inspection verified the provider/coordinator artifact fallback, the exact idle `Evaluate` call at four hertz, durable freshness rules, status projection precedence, store indexes, and affected tests. Alternatives that merely raise the cap, stream huge JSON, or cache detailed rows were rejected.

### IMPACT

No production code has changed. The medium-risk doubt-pass workflow selected one read-only Claude review, but the programmatic invocation may consume separate credits and therefore waits for explicit spend approval.
