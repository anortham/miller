## WHAT

Round 1 review fixes for Task 2: moving-cursor retries now re-probe the live revision at the poller boundary, and durable cursor methods are internal. Added forward-convergence and bounded-attempt tests.

## WHY

The previous implementation retried the impact session three times against the same stale `current` cursor. If the store advanced after the probe, all retries saw the old target and the poll stayed unavailable until a later tick. The cursor helpers also exceeded the approved internal seam scope.

## HOW

- `ContinuousTestRevisionPoller.PollAsync` owns `MaxRevisionReconcileAttempts = 3`. On `moving_cursor`, it calls `IContinuousTestRevisionSource.RefreshAsync` again, validates workspace/identity/freshness, returns rebuild/degraded/missing honestly when needed, and retries impact with the newly observed current cursor.
- `MillerFactImpactSource.ImpactAsync` now performs one full session read and returns `moving_cursor`; it no longer retries stale sessions or exposes an internal session-opening test seam.
- `ReadLastReconciledCursor` and `SaveLastReconciledCursor` are `internal`; Miller trace found only the poller and tests as callers, and `Miller.Testing` grants tests internals access.
- Added `Moving_cursor_reprobes_and_completes_the_forward_interval_in_one_poll` and `Moving_cursor_reconciliation_stops_after_three_total_attempts`.

## IMPACT

A store advance between probe and session can converge in the same `PollAsync` call while preserving the durable `from` cursor. Repeated drift is bounded and remains fail-closed. No public CLI/MCP surface changed. Task 7/8 files remain dirty and unstaged.
