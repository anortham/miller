## WHAT

Wrote separate QML indexing/resolution and continuous-testing designs plus five-task implementation plans under `docs/plans/2026-08-24-qml-*`.

## WHY

Miller currently indexes `.qml` generically but lacks module visibility rules, QML regression evidence, project discovery, and a Qt Quick Test provider.

## HOW

Indexing translates Julie import/qmldir/qmltypes facts into typed `QmlVisibleType` candidates with store/artifact parity and no global fallback. CT discovers strict topmost CMake QuickTest projects, requires CMake 3.21, uses CTest JSON v1 and JUnit XML, reuses shared runner/generation contracts, supports target-level selection, and rejects coverage explicitly. Cross-model objections about loader seams, implicit directory visibility, semantic corpus invariants, project-root collapse, complete output, version floors, and whole-suite contracts were incorporated.

## IMPACT

Miller now has two approval-ready, independently executable plans that preserve current public tools while making QML indexing and continuous testing honest first-class targets.
