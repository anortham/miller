## Repaired flaky sticky-unavailable daemon test

- **WHAT:** Replaced the fixed 1.5-second sleep in `CtStickyUnavailableDeltaTests.A_sticky_unavailable_delta_stops_the_four_hertz_loop_and_names_the_reason` with a real status-publication signal.
- **WHY:** CI could cancel before the eight required poll iterations and status publication under scheduler load.
- **HOW:** Added a `TaskCompletionSource` with `RunContinuationsAsynchronously`; the existing `StatusWriter` completes it only when the published reason contains both `impact unavailable` and `impact_truncated`. The test waits up to 30 seconds with `TestContext.Current.CancellationToken` and cancels/awaits the host in `finally`.
- **IMPACT:** The test now waits for the behavior it asserts and cleans up reliably on timeout or failure; no production code, workflow, or other test files changed.
