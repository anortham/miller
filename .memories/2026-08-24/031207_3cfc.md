## WHAT

Completed and lead-reviewed Task 2 of the CT performance hardening plan. `DotnetTestProvider` now uses xUnit verbose progress plus JUnit for whole-suite, no-coverage runs and returns an artifact-only provider result instead of parsing retained JSON stdout.

## WHY

The released whole-suite path retained and parsed tens of megabytes of xUnit JSON, exceeded the 8M-character safety cap, and failed after executing the suite. Verbose progress preserves the ten-minute silence/liveness guard while JUnit becomes the structured source of truth.

## HOW

Artifact-only eligibility is limited to xUnit whole-suite runs with no coverage. The provider validates a non-empty parser-readable JUnit artifact, accepts exit 0 only with a non-failed artifact and exit 1 only with a failed artifact, rejects other exits and flag/config failures with bounded diagnostics, and leaves selected/chunked/coverage/generic paths unchanged. Inline review corrected the old whole-suite contract and prevented arbitrary nonzero exits from being treated as test failures.

## IMPACT

Focused tests pass (provider 63/63; whole-suite contracts 4/4), the source project builds with zero warnings/errors, and the real pinned xUnit Scale proof passed 1/1 while producing importable JUnit.
