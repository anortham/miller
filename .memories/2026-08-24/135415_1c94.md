## WHAT

Completed the baseline Miller/QML and continuous-testing architecture audit.

## WHY

Miller advertises QML and now needs first-class proof plus an executable Qt Quick Test lane.

## HOW

- Live Miller search, inspect, trace, and patterns work over QML facts from Julie Extractors.
- Miller has no QML-specific fixture or regression test under its test/fixture trees.
- Resolution policy recognizes ES-module languages only; QML imports and qmldir metadata have no resolver policy.
- CT inventory discovers only .NET, Cargo, package.json, and Python project files.
- Default provider factory wires .NET, Rust, JavaScript, and Python; no CMake/CTest/Qt provider exists.
- Qt official docs verify Quick Test conventions and JUnit output; CTest offers stable JSON discovery and JUnit output.
- Native QML coverage is not a normal Quick Test artifact; Qt documents it through commercial Coco/CocoQML tooling.

## IMPACT

QML files are queryable today, but Miller cannot honestly claim QML-aware resolution or continuous testing.
