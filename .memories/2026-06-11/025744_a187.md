## Task 3: version-aware leadership orchestration in IndexerService (D2–D4)

**WHAT:** Wired the Task 1/2 pieces into `IndexerService`: claim gate, auto-upgrade rescan, yield protocol (both sides), and post-yield cooldown.

- **D2 claim gate:** every claim attempt first evaluates `LeadershipEligibility.Evaluate(ownVersion, artifactVersion, allowDowngrade)`; ineligible instances NEVER invoke the acquire func. Verdict surfaced on `internal LeadershipVerdict? EligibilityVerdict` for Task 4 status/health. Reason logged once at Information per transition, then Debug.
- **D3 auto-rescan:** on a claim whose verdict has `ArtifactOlderThanOwn`, one forced full scan via `ScanAsLeaderUnderGate(force:true, source:"extractor-upgrade")` right after the startup delta scan. `leader.json` now carries `ExtractorVersion`.
- **D4 requester:** reader retry tick sends `RequestYield` when eligible + live leader's `ExtractorVersion` strictly lower; at most one outstanding per (leader pid+StartedAtUtc) until `RequestTtl` or leader change.
- **D4 leader:** debounce tick drains `DrainYieldRequests` (decision evaluated first, acted on after the tick's work); strictly-newer challenger ⟹ abdicate (watchers/ops/core torn down, leader.json deleted, lease disposed, `MillerRole.SetReader`), then re-enter the claim loop via the new outer session loop (`RunLeadershipSessionAsync` returns true=abdicated, false=shutdown).
- **Cooldown:** new pure `YieldCooldown` (injected clock + pid-alive probe, 60s, clears permanently on expiry or requester death).

**HOW:** Extended the injectable-func internal ctor with 9 optional seams (yield drain, own version via `Lazy<string?>` so the production probe never runs in the ctor — host-lifecycle rule, allow-downgrade env, artifact reader, requestYield, leader identity reader, leader-alive probe, clock, pid probe). New `*ForTest` hooks: `AttemptClaimForTest`, `MaybeRequestYieldForTest`, `AssumeLeadershipForTest`, `ProcessYieldRequestsForTest`.

**IMPACT:** All 4 design decisions now live in the indexer loop; Task 4 (status/health render) can read `EligibilityVerdict`; Task 5 scale handoff test has the runtime behavior to exercise.
