## Completed empty-state recovery slice

- **WHAT:** Added `SymbolSuggestionEngine` and wired resolver/search empty states to suggest likely symbols for one-edit and camel-case suffix typos. Added focused tests for resolver near-miss recovery and search compact/JSON empty suggestions.
- **WHY:** The approved plan identified high-volume empty `inspect`/`search` misses as a major friction point, especially typo cases like `ReadReferencesAsyncc`.
- **HOW:** The engine combines existing search recall with generated one-edit exact-name variants and bounded Damerau-style edit distance. Search suggestions are only added for ordinary symbol misses, leaving scoped filtered misses and file-mode misses unchanged.
- **IMPACT:** Focused resolver and search suites are green. Next slice is the `metrics` surface for churn, clones, and complexity.
