## WHAT

Completed and lead-reviewed the BRE-57 lifecycle, content, and continuous-testing slice. `WorkspaceTool`, `ContentTool`, and `TestsTool` now operate from machine-global host paths and the registry when no primary workspace is bound. Selected operations stamp telemetry with the resolved workspace ID and root.

## WHY

A user-level MCP server cannot trust its launch cwd, and stateless clients may not preserve session binding. Lifecycle, content, and CT calls therefore must resolve each explicit selector independently without inheriting an ambient workspace.

## HOW

Added unbound construction paths, routed named selectors through `WorkspaceRegistrySelector`, kept `current`/`primary` compatibility only when a primary exists, preserved root and write-safety checks, and left registry-wide list/prune/dashboard plus `content workspace_id=all|registered` telemetry unscoped. Added caller-level telemetry persistence tests after review found the initial global ledger had no default workspace.

## IMPACT

Task 4 is complete and ready for host integration. Production DI still needs to choose these unbound constructors and retire startup Roots binding in Task 6.
