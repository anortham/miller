## Captured bridge-provider direction

Recorded the approved TODO 10 direction as a design artifact.

- **WHAT:** Added `docs/plans/2026-06-05-bridge-provider-scope-design.md` and updated `TODO.md` to route bridge work through a provider-scoped design.
- **WHY:** The existing bridge capability is valuable to coding agents, but the implementation is specific to ASP.NET + TS/JS/Vue URL literals + AutoMapper/EF and should not imply all-language support.
- **HOW:** The design keeps `BridgeGraph`, `BridgeScorer`, and `trace mode=bridge` generic; extracts today's reducers behind a `dotnet-web` provider; uses committed root `miller.json` for provider selection; and puts immutable capability metadata on `BridgeGraph` so unsupported/no-provider states are visible.
- **IMPACT:** Future bridge work can preserve trust by adding tested providers and explicit capability reporting instead of widening into vague semantic matching.
