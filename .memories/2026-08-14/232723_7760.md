## WHAT

Aborted the first final Task 8 Linux replay because the machine was not quiet enough for authoritative timing.

- Stopped only the isolated replay process group; unrelated Miller hosts, protected Miller PID 23058, semantic broker PID 24610, and the live store were untouched.
- Restored the staged workspace pointer to its original `copied-store` target.

## WHY

The recovery plan requires quiet Linux attempts. Four Miller servers were children of the active Codex session and one was consuming CPU in a live-store full resolve, so any timing result would be contaminated and could not close a hard performance gate.

The replay was interrupted before any strict JSONL/report was accepted. Read-only process inspection resolved the unrelated workload, then the exact replay PID was terminated and the Luna worker restored the pointer with `apply_patch`. No live process or live artifact was signaled or modified.
