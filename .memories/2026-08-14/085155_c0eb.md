## WHAT

Corrected the Task 1B snapshot contract after the first copied-store attempt aborted on source SHM churn.

## WHY

A reader advanced `gen-001/store.db-shm` during copying even though durable database state did not change. SQLite documents SHM as a transient wal-index with no database content; WAL, by contrast, is persistent state that must stay paired with the main database.

## HOW

- Revised the plan and active brief to copy and stability-check only main+WAL.
- The helper now lets SQLite rebuild SHM inside the private shadow and never copies or gates on source SHM.
- Added official SQLite contract links to the plan.
- Added RED/GREEN coverage for SHM churn, WAL preservation/mutation, source metadata stability, cleanup, and WAL/SHM-free destination.

## IMPACT

Task 1B-B can retry safely while read-only Miller sessions exist. Any main or WAL mutation still aborts before promotion.
