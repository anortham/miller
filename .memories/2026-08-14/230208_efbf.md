## WHAT

Fixed the Task 8 replay harness so `startup_total=skipped` is treated as a terminal phase outcome instead of waiting until the workload deadline.

## WHY

The staged scratch workspace carried a crash-recovery request journal for an old `parent_process_exited` producer result. Its first warmup retired that stale request, then persisted scan backoff emitted `startup_total=skipped`. `_McpSession.wait_for_phase` recognized only `completed` and `failed`, turning honest failure evidence into a 60-second apparent hang on each attempt.

## HOW

Added a live-process regression that writes a real skipped phase record and proves the wait returns without sleeping. The test failed before the change and passed after adding `skipped` to both terminal phase paths. No cleanup, retry, startup, or success semantics changed.

## IMPACT

Contaminated or throttled replay state now fails quickly and truthfully. After the stale scratch state converged, three measured exact startup runs completed in 1.286–1.288 seconds with roughly 75 MiB peak/idle PSS, proving the product startup path itself remains within budget.
