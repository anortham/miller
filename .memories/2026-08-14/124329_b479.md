## Accepted blank roots in setup reports

The performance-recovery harness rejected Julie's valid successful `store resolve --json` report when its view-scoped `root` field was empty.

- **WHAT:** Added a focused public-shape regression test and a two-line guard in `_validate_setup_result`.
- **WHY:** Empty or whitespace-only `root`, `root_path`, `workspace_root`, and `source_root` values represent unavailable evidence; canonicalizing them as paths falsely maps them to the process directory.
- **HOW:** Blank strings are treated as absent evidence; non-empty roots continue through the existing canonical workspace comparison and mismatch error.
