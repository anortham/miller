## WHAT

Hardened the performance recovery plan and active brief with a WAL-safe immutable snapshot contract.

## WHY

The incident coordinator store has a nonempty WAL, while checkpointing or otherwise writing the live family is forbidden. Rejecting all WAL-bearing sources would block faithful replay; raw copying could capture an inconsistent boundary.

## HOW

- Require stopped writers and reject live or unknown owners.
- Open each SQLite source read-only and use SQLite's backup API so committed WAL state is incorporated without writing or checkpointing the source.
- Require stable source identity/facts through backup, `quick_check` on destination databases, and no destination WAL/SHM files.
- Limit raw/reflink copies to WAL-free non-database files.

## IMPACT

Task 1B can safely reproduce the real incident state on Linux and Windows without risking the live store or losing committed WAL data.
