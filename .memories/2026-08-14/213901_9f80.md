## WHAT

Completed Task 7B's post-rotation eight-arm baseline and measured Miller query-shape repair.

- Added an internal per-call observation seam for the exact `ReadMany` statements and an opt-in cross-platform Scale JSONL harness.
- Captured reverse/forward × exact/fallback × 1/100-ID cold, warmup, and three-run warm evidence on the compacted Task 7A family.
- Forced target-first join order in the measured reverse fallback identifier join and the two reverse exact resolution-base joins.
- Added production-SQL plan contracts requiring the identifier-name and both resolution-target indexes.

## WHY

After lifecycle compaction, single-ID reverse fallback still scanned the identifier-name index at a 2,475.0633 ms median and single-ID reverse exact scanned the active resolution base at 145.5446 ms. The 100-ID forms sought the existing indexes, proving planner join order—not missing indexes or retained history—owned the remaining bounded-read defect.

## HOW

The repair uses SQLite's existing `CROSS JOIN ... ON` pattern to keep the materialized target set outside each affected join. No schema, index, statistics, API, MCP, result limit, or feature change was made. Baseline, intermediate, and final JSONL preserved exact plans, rows, timings, cache state, statement counts, and stable result digests.

## IMPACT

Single-ID reverse fallback fell to 0.9536 ms (-99.96%) and reverse exact to 1.3852 ms (-99.05%). All reverse base scans are gone; the slowest final cell is the 100-ID reverse fallback at 943.4084 ms. Every corresponding baseline/final raw-row count, returned-row count, and stable result digest matches. The full fast boundary passed 6,555 tests with 4 expected skips.
