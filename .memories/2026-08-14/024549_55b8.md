## WHAT

Completed a read-only performance investigation on `main` at `321e546282df03fd077297a659fb5c3eb45d44d5` covering warm startup, leader startup work, `context`/`impact`/`inspect`/`trace`, family-store storage/query shape, julie-extract coordination, and Windows-specific risks.

## WHY

The project was operational again but had regressed from making agents faster to imposing multi-second and multi-minute waits. The investigation needed measured attribution before any optimization work.

## HOW

- Reproduced a fixed `context` workload after all workers stopped: depth 0 was 1.386/1.378/1.375 seconds; depth 1 was 11.922/11.930/11.936 seconds, with byte-identical 2,665-byte output.
- Telemetry shows `ContextTool.BuildReferenceItems` enriches every candidate before token packing. Other workloads spent about 5.95 seconds in `bundle` and returned no reference items.
- Reproduced `impact` at about 2.2 seconds: 52 candidates and thousands of family resolution/completion rows were expanded before a 20-result limit.
- Verified warm reader startup is about 0.33 seconds; the live leader's startup delta/convergence took about 28.5 seconds.
- Found a producer coordinator import stuck `claimed` under a dead PID with an expired lease and an unbound view.
- Coordinator history records 100 resolves totaling 25,519 seconds, 255.2 seconds average, 1,252.3 seconds maximum. Recent full/crossover exact resolution phases took 163.6–171.9 seconds.
- Verified the 2.44 GB store is live data, not freelist fragmentation. Resolution overlay tables/indexes occupy about 1.12 GB; exact-gap JSON is about 352 MB.
- Query plans show indexed delta paths but full scans in base overlay arms; no `sqlite_stat1/stat4` is present.
- Ruled out raw tree-sitter extraction, jobs, OOM, semantic broker, connection creation, freshness resolution, and content-chunk indexing as the primary causes of the measured failures.

## IMPACT

The recovery path is now ordered by evidence: repair the stranded coordinator state; stop resolving unchanged/reused manifests; complete incremental resolution and compact/rebase retained overlays; enrich only context candidates that survive packing using batched exact reads; cap impact graph work before output limits; then add phase/count telemetry and Windows-specific soak gates.
