## Durable SQLite snapshot inputs ignore source SHM churn

- **WHAT:** Changed the snapshot helper to track and stream only each database's main file and WAL; source `-shm` files are excluded from family enumeration and never copied into private shadows.
- **WHY:** SQLite WAL shared-memory state is reconstructed and may legitimately change while a stopped store is being read; treating source `-shm` metadata/content as durable caused safe snapshots to abort.
- **HOW:** `_database_state` and `_database_input` now use the main/WAL pair. `_source_files` skips `-shm` before alias/reparse checks. Added disposable WAL fixtures proving SHM content/mtime churn is tolerated, no SHM is streamed or promoted, durable main/WAL facts stay unchanged, and same-size WAL mutations still fail.
- **IMPACT:** Committed WAL rows remain captured through the private shadow while the source is not opened, checkpointed, chmodded, or mutated. Existing owner gates, cleanup, destination validation, and atomic promotion remain unchanged.
