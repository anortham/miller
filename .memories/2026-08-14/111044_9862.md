## Real-store retry conflict correction

### WHAT

Corrected the bounded recovery harness's retry identity and failed-producer handling.

### WHY

A retained copied coordinator already contained the fixed `perf-recovery-import` request, so retry warmup hit an idempotency conflict. The harness then raised while demanding a generation from Julie's failed JSON report (`manifest.generation: null`) instead of preserving a failed measured row.

### HOW

- Replaced the fixed retry request/idempotency values in the manifest with `{retry_identity}`.
- Generate one Windows-safe `perf-recovery-retry-<uuidhex>` identity per isolated workload and preserve it across warmup and measured attempts.
- Allow retry reports to bind to the source view while still requiring the staged family/store pointer itself to remain unchanged.
- Detect Julie failure state/failure class/coordinator/error evidence and bypass successful-result adoption checks for failed, timed-out, non-completed, or failed-JSON producer results.
- Preserve failure facts in record metadata, keep null generation null, and force the hard gate false.
- Successful producer reports still require view and generation adoption; generation uses the selected view's durable catalog fact when available.

### IMPACT

Retry rows no longer collide with coordinator history copied into disposable stores. Failed Julie reports become measured evidence rather than harness exceptions, while successful binding/view/generation safety remains enforced.
