## WHAT

Documented the validated store-pointer adoption design and ADR required to unblock faithful copied-family leader startup.

## WHY

Root-cause tracing proved direct CLI and warm-reader paths trust a valid pointer, while a lock-winning leader with an empty isolated registry ignores it and creates a new family. The harness also conflated Julie's original source root with Miller's staged root.

## HOW

- Pointer adoption remains inside the store coordinator/resolver and uses existing family read validation before any registry mutation.
- Replay gains separate source and staged-root roles.
- Added the design, ADR-0005, docs map entries, and a dependency step in the recovery plan.

## IMPACT

This preserves incident family history without a perf-only bypass or live pointer mutation and makes CLI, warm-reader, and leader binding behavior consistent.
