- **HOW:** `DotnetWebBridgeProvider` now reduces only real route-reference patterns (`htmx`, `vue`, `react`, `nextjs`, `nuxt`) as client calls, excludes file-route and route-definition facts, and skips when no backend evidence exists. - **IMPACT:** Pure Next.js fixture repos leave `dotnet-web` inactive while `nextjs` remains active, hybrid frontend-to-ASP.NET edges still work, and shared route observations dedupe across providers.
