## Task 5 navigation verb honesty complete

- **WHAT:** Changed structural navigation route references to carry unknown verbs unless htmx supplies an attested HTTP verb.
- **WHY:** Vue/React/Next/Nuxt navigation facts were synthesized as GET calls, which incorrectly produced verb-known High confidence edges to ASP.NET GET endpoints.
- **HOW:** `StructuralRouteFactAdapter` now returns `Verb = null` for non-htmx route references and still requires htmx verb resolution. `DotnetWebBridgeProvider.StructuralCarrier` emits verbless carriers for navigation references, letting `RouteNormalizer.FromClientCall` produce `VerbKnown=false` and `RouteBridge` emit `RouteOnlyMatch`.
- **IMPACT:** Navigation references still produce frontend-to-endpoint edges by route, but they score Medium with `IsVerbUnknown`; htmx verb-attested edges remain High.
