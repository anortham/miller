## Completed Next.js bridge trace implementation

Miller now supports pure Next.js `trace mode=bridge` over `julie-extractors` structural facts, without requiring ASP.NET backend evidence.

- **WHAT:** Added the reusable structural-route adapter, the `nextjs` bridge provider, Next route matching, `navigates_to` edges, `next_route` nodes, route-string diagnostics, and docs/agent guidance updates.
- **WHY:** The bridge graph needed to consume `nextjs.route_reference.v1` and `nextjs.file_route.v1` facts so Next apps can dogfood route-reference-to-file-route traces directly in Miller.
- **HOW:** Kept parser recognition in `julie-extractors`; Miller consumes stable `structural_facts`, wires `nextjs` into default provider selection, keeps explicit `miller.json` provider lists authoritative, and renders Next navigation separately from ASP.NET HTTP hits.
- **IMPACT:** This establishes the reusable provider pattern for future framework support and gives both seeded fast coverage and real extractor-backed scale coverage for Next.js.
