## HOW

- RunAsync consumes GodotProjectShadow.Sync, NeedsImport, PublishImportStamp, TouchActivity, MapSourcePath, and EnforcePostProcessBudget.
- Godot is invoked with --version, headless import, and headless GUT batch flags; every process is followed by activity touch and post-process budget enforcement.
- GUT config is derived under the mirror-owned results directory, unknown settings are preserved, and runner-owned settings are fixed.
- JUnit output is copied atomically into the CT results path and parsed fail-closed, including inner-class attribution, duplicate/unselected/missing-row checks, and exit-code agreement.
- Existing ProviderCaseResult.Metadata carries elapsed timings, candidate sizes, and mirror counters needed by Task 3; no public contract was added.
