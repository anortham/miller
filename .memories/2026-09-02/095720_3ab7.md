## WHAT

Wrote the proposed high-risk Task 7 adaptation at `docs/plans/2026-09-02-sbt-ct-workspace-shadow-design.md` after comparing three independent architecture lanes.

## WHY

The official sbt probe invalidated in-place execution, but the user goal still requires runnable sbt support without performance regressions. The design must contain unavoidable sbt writes while preserving warm Zinc state.

## HOW

The chosen provider-local mirror lives under the existing project-stable CT cache, reconciles only changed source-owned entries, retains build-owned targets/caches, runs sbt from the mirror, and copies JUnit reports into generation results. It keeps `IJvmTestBackend`, `CtGenerationPaths`, and public contracts unchanged. It adds an isolated Git-discovery barrier, safe-link rules, atomic manifests, duplicate-class refusal, Windows/Scale gates, and cold/warm sync metrics. A one-call Claude doubt pass was attempted but the outbound redactor failed before dispatch; the campaign closed blocked with zero external invocations and no repository content sent.

## IMPACT

Production implementation is approval-gated by the brainstorming workflow. Once approved, Task 7 can be replanned into TDD implementation plus performance/Windows verification, followed by Tasks 8–9 and the release gates.
