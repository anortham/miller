## WHAT

Corrected Task 4 PHP discovery and filter contracts. Added `PhpListTestsXmlParser` for PHPUnit 10 `<tests>/<testCaseClass>/<testCaseMethod>` and PHPUnit 11/12 namespaced `<testSuite>/<tests>/<testClass>/<testMethod>` listings, and updated provider discovery to use it. Anchored PHP filters and escaped `/`, with conservative chunk-cost accounting.

## WHY

The prior provider incorrectly parsed `--list-tests-xml` as JUnit result XML, which rejected real PHPUnit 10+ reports. Its unanchored regex filters could select `testAddExtra` when `testAdd` was requested, and `/` was not escaped for PHPUnit’s filter handling.

## HOW

The listing parser is pure and uses a prohibiting XML reader with no resolver. Method `id` is authoritative: it is split at the first `::`, its class must match the enclosing class, and the complete method/data-set tail is retained. Run artifacts continue to use `JUnitXmlResultParser` only. Primary-source-shaped PHPUnit 10 and 12 fixtures, a data-set case, class-mismatch and DTD tests, and a prefix/slash filter regression exercise the corrected surface. Focused PHP/parser verification passed; the PHP Scale smoke skipped because PHP/PHPUnit are absent; the Release build passed with zero warnings/errors.

## IMPACT

Real PHPUnit 10+ and Pest 2-compatible discovery is now supported, and selected filters cannot overmatch prefix-colliding methods. The correction is ready for lead inline review. No release or push was attempted.
