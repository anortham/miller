## Fixed H1: re-plan after gate-time freshness recovery + L2 poll hardening (EditService)

**WHAT:** When the apply-path freshness gate found the index stale and `TryRecoverFreshness` succeeded, `FinishSingleFile`/`ExecuteRename` applied the PRE-recovery plan whose byte spans came from the stale index — prepend drift (lines added above a symbol) made the splice silently rewrite the wrong bytes.

**HOW:**
- Threaded an `allowRecovery` flag through `ExecuteSingleFile` → `PlanAndFinishSingleFile` → `FinishSingleFile` and into `ExecuteRename`. After a successful recovery, symbol ops re-run the resolve → span read → plan pipeline once (`allowRecovery: false` — the retry never re-enters recovery; a stale verdict there refuses via `StaleBlocked`). `replace_text` plans derive spans from disk content, so they apply unchanged after recovery.
- Rename: tracked `anyRecovered` across the per-file gate loop (multi-stale-file recovery still shares one budget), then restarted `ExecuteRename` once with recovery disabled so identifier/def-token sites are re-read from the converged index.
- L2: the in-poll `FreshnessGate.Check` inside `TryRecoverFreshness` now catches transient `SqliteException`/`FileNotFoundException`/`InvalidOperationException` as "not yet fresh" and keeps polling within budget — `Execute` keeps its never-throws promise during a mid-converge DB swap.

**TESTS (TDD, red proven by stashing the fix):**
- `Execute_StaleTarget_PrependDrift_RecoveryConverges_AppliesAtConvergedOffsets` — byte-exact post-apply content at SHIFTED offsets.
- `Execute_RenameSymbol_PrependDrift_RecoveryConverges_RewritesAtConvergedOffsets` — rename re-plans sites from the converged index.
- `Execute_StaleTarget_RecoveryPoll_SurvivesThrowingGateCheck_ThenApplies` — DB moved aside mid-poll (FileNotFoundException) then restored converged; no escape from Execute.
- New `ShiftIndexedSpans` test helper simulates a real single-file converge moving symbol+identifier byte/line offsets (NULL spans stay NULL).

**IMPACT:** Closes the one high-severity finding from the fa78c49 review — recovered-fresh edits can no longer splice at stale offsets and corrupt files. Fast suite 1916/1916 green.
