## Implemented fixes 3 & 4: agent-speed freshness timings + leader visibility in workspace health

**WHAT (fix 3):** Tightened the read-your-writes chain — `IndexerService.DebounceInterval` 1s → 250ms, `FreshnessService.PollInterval` 2s → 500ms. New `FreshnessLatencyBudgetTests` pins the invariant: debounce + 2×poll + 1s extract allowance ≤ the edit gate's 2.5s recovery budget, so nobody can relax a constant past the budget unnoticed. Worst-case reader convergence drops from ~5s to ~1.25s + extract.

**WHAT (fix 4):** Leader identity surfaced in `workspace health`. The leader writes `.miller/leader.json` (pid, version, process path, started-at) on winning the writer lock and deletes it on graceful step-down (before releasing the lock). Health reads it + probes pid liveness (`LeaderIdentityFile.IsProcessAlive`) and emits: `indexer_leader_unknown` (no identity, not leader — older build leading or none), `indexer_leader_dead` (degraded — recorded pid not running), `indexer_leader_version_mismatch` (live leader on a different build, with process path). Rendered as a `leader:` line (compact) and an `indexer_leader` JSON block; wired through WorkspaceTool (current + target workspace) and all three CLI health paths; documented additively in `docs/contracts/workspace-health-v1.md`.

**VERIFIED:** 18 new tests RED→GREEN; fast suite 1892/1892; scale 25/25. Real-world smoke: `miller workspace health` on this repo correctly reports "leader: unknown (no leader identity recorded — older build leading...)" because the live leader is the 0.3.6 plugin-cache binary — exactly the diagnosis that was missing this morning.

**NOTE:** Leader pid/version/alive only populate once servers restart on a build that writes leader.json.
