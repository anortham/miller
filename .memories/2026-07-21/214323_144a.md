## Dogfood night-1 bug: content-corpus zombie sources wedge the chunk vector lane

**WHAT:** The docs/chunk vector lane on the primary miller workspace never converged under the new bge pin. Three-part root cause, all verified in source: (1) ContentCorpusWriter has NO source-retirement path — deleted files stay `active` forever; (2) out-of-extractor-scope files (`.trx` under TestResults/) never bump the workspace revision, so the corpus converge never re-examines them — even `workspace full` leaves the zombies; (3) EvaluateChunkCursor defers forever on an active source with no symbols.db hash. Full write-up: docs/findings/2026-07-21-content-corpus-zombie-sources-chunk-wedge.md.

**WHY:** First-night bge dogfooding after the pin swap; chunk_completed_revision pinned at 0 with per-cycle deferral logs.

**HOW:** Unwedged locally by deleting the trx files and manually retiring the two zombie rows (`status='missing'` — the corpus's active-only joins make this equivalent to correct retirement), then a serve round to rebuild vectors for the post-full-rebuild artifact id. Product fix (corpus retirement sweep + gate distinction + TestResults scope exclusion) deliberately deferred to a tested change.

**IMPACT:** Also logged for follow-up: CLI `refresh --workspace` false `lock_busy`, stale leader-identity `alive` reporting, and — my own error — SIGTERM'd the user's codex miller server after misreading a `grep "miller serve"` inventory (codex launches with no args; match on the binary, not the verb).
