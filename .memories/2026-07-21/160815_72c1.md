## Build-time embedding throughput logging added

**WHAT:** `VectorConvergeService` now times its two embed paths: the incremental drain logs "Vector {Cursor} convergence embedded {Count} unit(s) in {ElapsedMs} ms" (only when count > 0), and the shadow-rebuild promote line gains "in {ElapsedMs} ms ({CardsPerSecond:F0} cards/s)". Two Stopwatches, one file.

**WHY:** User-approved follow-up from the P5 canary enablement: query-time embed latency was in telemetry (bucketed), but build-time sidecar throughput was completely unmeasured — the 10k-card rebuild produced no timing evidence, notable given the 2026-07-20 "sidecar 8x slow" finding. Sidecar stderr is discarded by contract, so Miller-side timing is the only signal.

**HOW:** TDD via the existing `RecordingLogger` surface in `VectorConvergeServiceTests` — two failing tests first (`Drain_IncrementalEmbed_LogsTheUnitCountAndElapsedMilliseconds`, `Drain_ShadowRebuildPromote_LogsTheBuildDurationAndThroughput`), then the implementation. Rate guards zero-elapsed with `Math.Max(seconds, 0.001)`. Quick-fix tier (fixing-small-issues): 1 source file, ~15 lines, log-only, no contract change.

**IMPACT:** Future rebuilds and converge batches leave cards/sec evidence in `.miller/logs/`, giving the model-comparison phase (qwen3 vs bge-small vs julie's CodeRankEmbed baseline) free throughput data. Verified: VectorConvergeServiceTests 46/46, Release 0W/0E (live serving binary refreshed in place).
