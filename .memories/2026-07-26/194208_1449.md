## Revised the cross-repo dogfood repair contract after independent review

The first specification missed real producer-consumer invariants and incorrectly treated Eros compatibility as a concern.

- **What:** Revised `docs/plans/2026-07-26-cross-repo-dogfood-contract-repair-design.md` to resolve all validated Claude findings.
- **Why:** Reference identity, semantic admission, capability-gap counts, continuation identity, and Eros contract ownership were not precise enough to implement correctly.
- **How:** Made reference sites producer-attested target-token spans; keyed assertions by site, target, and kind; separated semantic routing from admission; used population fingerprints and discriminated cursors; specified per-line marker facts; pinned the current 70-row open-gap invariant; required authoritative schema fixtures, pin restore, and full rebuild; and made Eros an atomic breaking consumer update.
- **Impact:** The design now fixes structural causes without legacy adapters. Implementation remains approval-gated.
