## Implemented semantic admission policy v2

Separated query-shape routing from lexical-population admission and wired the decision through Miller symbol/content/context/CLI/canary paths plus the offline fusion evaluator.

- **Why:** The v1 `LexicalEvidence.IsStrong` rule treated a sole lexical hit as decisive because its runner-up was zero, blocking semantic recall and conflating routing with admission.
- **How:** `Route(query)` is shape-only; `DecideAdmission(evidence)` implements zero-hit expansion, protected one-hit expansion, decisive positive-runner-up rerank-only, and weak multi-hit expansion.
- **Versioning:** `SemanticQueryPolicy.PolicyVersion` is the integer `2`; canary call/shadow facts require explicit wiring, and fusion-eval JSONL rows emit `policy_version: 2`.
- **Serving:** Symbol and content fusion exclude semantic-only rows under decisive evidence and preserve the sole lexical winner; context, forced CLI, shadow, and eval reuse the same decision.
- **Impact:** Corrects the degenerate one-hit behavior without changing RRF weights or lexical-only routing bytes.
