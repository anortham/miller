### Findings (mine, pre-second-pass)
- **Important — seeded shadow inherits `build_state="ready"` and the live symbol completed cursor.** Fresh stores are born `building` (VectorStore.cs:615) and flip to ready only at cursor completion; `EvaluateBuildState` is sticky-ready. `BuildShadowAsync`'s meta reset block clears artifact_id/chunk/error/pause keys but NOT `build_state` or the symbol completedKey — so a half-built seeded shadow claims ready-from-birth, breaking the invariant `RecoverInterruptedPromote` (VectorGenerationManager.cs:251) relies on ("ready ⟹ build completed"; adoption when active is missing). Fix: stamp `build_state=building` and completed=0 in the seed reset block.
- **Important (approach) — silent level downgrade on routine upgrade.** Under the new worktree default, `LevelForScan` rewrites any existing FULL worktree artifact at symbols level on the next FORCE — and extractor-upgrade rescans are automatic forces on every version bump. Existing worktree workspaces lose rename/trace-refs/impact/patterns without user action. Mirrors the "binary_version never goes backwards" principle in reverse; should be a conscious call.
- **Minor — GitWorktreeLayoutTests new asserts call the env-reading `Resolve` overload**, so `MILLER_INDEX_LEVELS`/`MILLER_FULL_REBUILD_INPLACE` set on a dev machine breaks the fast suite (suite convention is env-independent tests).
- **Minor — BootstrapScanLockScaleTests rewrite** (deterministic hook replaces 1.5s-delay publisher) loses the proof that the lock-wait loop POLLS mid-wait; an entry-time-only recheck would now pass.
