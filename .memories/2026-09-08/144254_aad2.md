## WHAT

Diagnosed the live semantic convergence delay without changing runtime state or source. The lexical index is fresh at revision 7456, while the vector sidecar is performing a full regeneration.

## WHY

The user expected semantic convergence in seconds and needed evidence distinguishing retry delay, locking, database cost, embedding time, and broker contention.

## HOW

- The first symbol shadow rebuild began at 09:25:56.429 and failed at 09:25:58.164 because family-store reader retention returned `Busy`.
- There was no vector retry at 09:30:58. Chunk convergence was already doing active embedding and completed 1,999 units at 09:32:26.723 after 388,348 ms.
- A new symbol shadow rebuild began at 09:32:28.330. It progressed from 18,000 symbol rows at 09:39:04 to 24,000 at 09:42:21. The fresh store contains 27,003 eligible symbol cards, so this is nearly complete full regeneration rather than a small delta.
- Embedding uses sequential 64-text broker calls; the 1,999-chunk timing covers 32 calls and excludes database commit time.
- The shared Vulkan broker is PID 67506, using Intel UHD 770 through `/dev/dri/renderD128`. It is owned by a Miller process in `/home/murphy/source/julie` and has seven connected Miller clients.
- Julie's own leader was continuously embedding overlapping work during this rebuild, directly establishing shared-broker queue contention.

## IMPACT

The pending state is explained by full corpus regeneration plus concurrent use of the one shared accelerator broker. Seconds is a reasonable expectation for a small warm delta, not for rebuilding 1,999 chunks and 27,003 symbol cards under concurrent load. The initial retention failure is real but is not the dominant elapsed cost.
