## WHAT

Resolved the apparent post-restart semantic stall as expected full-regeneration work after the database reset, not a five-minute idle retry.

## WHY

The initial diagnosis focused on a transient family-store reader `Busy` error and the coordinator's five-minute held retry. Later timestamps showed the coordinator was continuously doing useful chunk and symbol embedding work instead of waiting.

## HOW

- The chunk embedding log measured 1,999 units in 388,348 ms (5.15 chunks/s).
- Source inspection proved that stopwatch covers only `EmbedAsync`: sequential 64-item broker/session calls plus response processing; corpus reads, planning, and SQLite commit are outside it.
- Runtime measurement found 27,003 eligible symbol cards and 1,999 chunks on Intel UHD 770 Vulkan, with symbols running around 45 cards/s.
- The live store vector artifact began empty after the reset. It held 0 symbols and 1,999 chunks; the active shadow rebuild had reached 22,000 symbols during inspection.
- `DrainAsync` intentionally keeps symbol and chunk cursors independent. The transient Symbol shadow-open `Busy` let the chunk lane make progress; it did not create a five-minute idle gap.

## IMPACT

No retry-timing source change is justified. Lowering the generic held delay would not reduce the dominant full-regeneration cost. Normal delta convergence can still complete in seconds because it embeds only changed units; deleting/resetting the family store discards reusable store-side vector state and requires the full corpus again.
