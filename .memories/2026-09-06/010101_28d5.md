## M3: Fact-Cache Resource Accounting and Owned Lease Model Complete

Implemented M3 (Tasks 1-5) on branch `feature/fact-cache-resource-accounting` in worktree `/home/murphy/source/miller/.worktrees/fact-cache-resource-accounting` (commit `f8dbf3a5`).

### WHAT
- Defined `CacheResourceSnapshot` and `CacheResourceState` providing union-based accounting of unique retained, active, evicted-held, and oversized cache objects.
- Implemented `RevisionFactCacheLease` ensuring safe reader lifetime ownership with `Interlocked` double-dispose protection and callback release.
- Added stale-loader and concurrent-load token fencing in `RevisionFactCacheStore.Acquire`.
- Migrated `FamilyStoreReadSession` to hold `RevisionFactCacheLease`, closing all SQLite handles before producer registration release in `Dispose`. Completely deleted legacy bare-cache `GetOrAdvance`.
- Added `FactCacheResourceAccountingTests` verifying full/bounded parity across eviction, revision switch, duplicate loads, and oversized entries on real SQLite fixtures.
- Created `scripts/bench-fact-cache-resources.sh` benchmarking resource accounting with deterministic counters and separate process RSS sampling.
- Updated `docs/known-limits.md`, `docs/plans/2026-09-04-architecture-review-program.md`, and `docs/plans/2026-09-04-fact-cache-resource-accounting.md`.

### WHY
- Previously, resolution fact caches were handed out as bare objects without lifetime tracking. Evicted caches held by long-lived readers escaped memory accounting, and stale loader completions could resurrect older cache states.

### HOW
- Scopes track monotonic tokens to discard outdated loader completions.
- Active caches are reference-counted by object identity, ensuring multiple leases over the same cache contribute only once to live bytes.
- Benchmark samples process RSS separately from the process sampler (`Process.GetCurrentProcess().WorkingSet64`), never inferring RSS from cache estimates.
