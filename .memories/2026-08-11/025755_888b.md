## Prepared Miller 1.18.1 for publication

### WHAT

- Merged the family-store repair history and the zero-delta search/content sidecar metadata fast-forward into the release branch.

- Pinned the now-live `julie-extract` 2.31.4 release with its four verified checksums.
- Added release notes, producer adoption evidence, the serialized release plan, and an exact gitleaks suppression for one historical fixture false positive.

### WHY

The default versioned store was correct but cold extraction spent excessive time rebuilding identifier resolution, while a no-change Miller converge rewrote about 488 MB of sidecars. The release ships both producer and consumer repairs together.

### HOW

- `julie-extract` 2.31.4 derives resolution from the canonical persisted store and reduced the same Miller corpus from 6m20.55s to 2m24.54s with exact output.
- Miller fast-forwards search/content sidecar metadata when revision deltas are empty instead of rewriting unchanged payloads.
- The existing schema and MCP surface remain unchanged.

### IMPACT

Miller 1.18.1 is ready to commit and publish from the isolated clean worktree. The user's unrelated dirty main-checkout files remain untouched.
