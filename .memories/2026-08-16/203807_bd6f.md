## WHAT

Closed two Miller state-machine gaps found by the requested Grok final review.

- Background prime now reconciles `lock_busy` and `ineligible_extractor`: a still-Refreshing row with a readable DB atomically becomes LoadedExisting; without a DB it atomically becomes Error.
- MCP status/health no longer rewrites an existing Error row with no DB to Missing.
- Conditional transitions preserve concurrent Ready, LoadedExisting, Error, and Missing states.

## WHY

An accepted asynchronous open could otherwise remain Refreshing forever when refresh deliberately made no registry mutation, and a later status call could erase the useful prime failure.

## HOW

Added `WorkspaceRegistry.MarkLoadedExistingIfRefreshing`, terminal-result reconciliation in `WorkspaceOpenPrimeService`, and focused regressions for readable/absent terminal outcomes, concurrent state preservation, and Error-without-DB status/health.
