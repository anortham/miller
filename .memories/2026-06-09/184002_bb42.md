## Consumed julie-extractors v2.2.0 in Miller

### WHAT
Miller now pins `julie-extract` v2.2.0 and accepts the v3 SQLite/report/extract contract.

- Updated `MillerExtractContract` and `scripts/julie-pins.json` to v2.2.0 / schema 3 / contract 3 / report 3 using live GitHub release SHA-256 digests.
- Added v3 `structural_facts` and `complexity_metrics` tables to `JulieDbFixture` and renamed the fixture schema guard to current-schema wording.
- Extended `WorkspaceHealthReader` and `workspace health --json` with additive `extraction_quality.structural_facts` and `extraction_quality.complexity_metrics` aggregate sections.
- Updated `TODO.md`, `docs/contracts/workspace-health-v1.md`, and `docs/plans/2026-06-09-miller-data-opportunities-plan.md` to record the upstream handoff and remaining future query/ranking slices.

### WHY
`julie-extractors` v2.2.0 completed the upstream parser-backed facts that had been parked outside Miller: structural facts, complexity metrics, and clone-ready body hash semantics. Miller needed to consume the new artifact contract before Eros or agents can safely build on those facts.

### HOW
Kept this as a consume-first slice rather than inventing ranking or clone workflows:

- Exact contract bump to v3.
- Cheap SQLite aggregates in workspace health only.
- No Miller-owned thresholds, scores, or extractor-policy duplication.
- Future structural search, complexity ranking, and clone discovery remain separate designs.

### IMPACT
Agents and Eros can now discover that v3 structural/complexity facts exist through a stable Miller public surface. The richer product workflows can build on Miller contracts later without reading private SQLite internals.
