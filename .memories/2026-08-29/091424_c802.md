## WHAT

Wrote the light implementation plan for direction-aware impact reads at `docs/plans/2026-08-29-direction-aware-impact-reads.md`.

## WHY

The approved design needs an exact TDD scope and a measurement protocol that cannot mix startup-inclusive CLI timing with resident-process latency.

## HOW

The plan has two serialized tasks. Task 1 changes only `QueryTimeResolutionReader` and its focused tests. Task 2 rebuilds and runs a one-shot parity check followed by one cold and five warm resident calls. A 25 percent p95 improvement is required to keep the change. A 5-second p95 closes the product gate. Failed parity or performance routes a revert rather than leaving speculative code.

## IMPACT

The implementation cannot add storage, caches, kind-specific partial scratch, or lazy detail loading. Non-graph inbound and outgoing readers retain their full bidirectional behavior.
