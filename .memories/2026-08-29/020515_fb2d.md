## WHAT

Wrote the approved direction-aware impact read design in `docs/plans/2026-08-28-direction-aware-impact-reads-design.md`.

## WHY

Reverse impact currently builds a bidirectional query scratch and spends 3.603 seconds reading forward-only identifier and pending rows that neither graph consumer uses.

## HOW

The design passes the existing `Direction` into `ResolveQuery`. Forward reads load within/source arms, reverse reads load named arms, and `Both` retains all arms. Scratch still contains the union needed by both graph consumer kinds, so reuse stays independent of call order. Resolver policy, detail loading, relationships, edge order, and public output do not change.

The change must improve warm resident p95 by at least 25 percent to remain. The product target is 5 seconds. Lazy detail loading remains a separate conditional design.

## IMPACT

The design removes the largest confirmed unused read without adding storage, cache invalidation, a producer change, or a new public contract. Architecture risk is medium because partial scratch could otherwise make edges depend on which consumer runs first.
