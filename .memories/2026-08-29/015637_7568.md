## WHAT

Completed and lead-reviewed the impact read-path diagnostic spike. The branch now has internal resolution subphase telemetry, a fixed replay, a same-connection SQLite comparison, and a documented architecture verdict.

## WHY

The proposed reference sidecar would add storage and lifecycle complexity. The spike needed to prove whether visibility lookup was large enough to justify that cost.

## HOW

The existing graph statement observer records candidate lookup, within-symbol and named arms, detail hydration, resolver loops, and relationship reads. The fixed workload produced byte-identical output and stayed under the instrumentation overhead gate. A read-only SQLite experiment compared the live manifest join with a connection-local TEMP visibility projection and confirmed equal rows.

The lead review split the recommendation into an ordered measurement ladder. First implement direction/consumer-aware graph reads without changing resolver or detail behavior, then replay. Only if the 5-second gate remains open should a separate lazy-detail experiment begin.

## IMPACT

The sidecar is rejected as the next change. The measured target is 3.603 seconds of unused within-symbol work on reverse reads. Proof traversal can amplify this through repeated resolution passes, while named visibility accounts for only 498 milliseconds. The current product gate remains open.
