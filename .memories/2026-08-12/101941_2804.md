## WHAT

Added an indexed empty-intersection probe for multi-term FTS `SearchMode.And` requests.

- Empty intersections return before uncapped candidate hydration, C# tokenization, scoring, and discard.
- Non-empty intersections fall through to the existing word-candidate and ranking body unchanged.
- Internal completion-only observations expose the probe and word-candidate families for deterministic tests; the public `Open` contract is unchanged.

## WHY

The rebuilt candidate proved two AND searches consumed 416 ms and returned zero rows. The old disk implementation executed an OR union over every symbol matching any term, then discovered in C# that no candidate contained every term.

## HOW

The probe uses the same distinct terms and existing FTS quoting, joined with `AND`, and performs `SELECT 1 ... LIMIT 1`. Tests cover 2,000 disjoint high-fanout symbols, intersecting ranking/score parity against the in-memory implementation, reserved FTS syntax, embedded quotes, and non-ASCII input.

## IMPACT

The proven zero-result AND work becomes a bounded index existence lookup while byte-for-byte ranking behavior for any non-empty AND result remains on the established path. No schema, public API, result limit, or OR behavior changed.
