## Performance bottleneck ledger and first Miller read-path milestone

- **WHAT:** Created root `PERF.md` with explicit latency/memory budgets and tracked PERF-001 through PERF-010. Accepted the disk-backed family-store read path at `dabcddd7` and its lazy bridge-parity follow-up at `1fa03ac9`.
- **WHY:** Miller hosts were retaining 1-2GB each, warm context/trace/impact took about 6 seconds, and idle freshness work produced 101.5GB logical reads. A durable ledger prevents each newly discovered bottleneck from being lost while another is fixed.
- **HOW:** Family reads now use the FTS sidecar and pinned-session SQLite graph rather than `RepositoryIndexLoader.LoadSession`; bridge data is loaded once only when bridge mode is invoked. Julie gained fixed 14-family query telemetry at `bdf2076c`; two suspected amplification families were disproven. A single worktree registration attempt timed out, continued an extractor past 90 seconds at about 115% CPU/1.8GB RSS, and was terminated once without retry.
- **IMPACT:** 361/361 affected Miller tests pass in 0.96s test time. PERF-002 lazy bootstrap/freshness work is active. Julie's faithful replay setup hit its 60-second limit and will not be repeated unchanged. Release remains blocked on measured budgets, but every remaining blocker and gate is durable.
