## WHAT

Added seven fixed, request-correlated measurements to source-rescue content search: connection, per-term document frequency, strict candidates, widened candidates, full hydration, scoring/mapping, and final ordering.

## WHY

Candidate context remains just over 2 s and source rescue varies from ~0.6–0.86 s, now the largest phase. Existing outer timing cannot select SQL, hydration, or scoring.

## HOW

Internal completion observations feed a nested AsyncLocal collector activated by ReadPhaseTelemetry. Context emits aggregate delta/total at the existing correlated source-rescue boundary. Observer work is excluded from measured elapsed. No query data, public API, schema, or per-row logs.

## IMPACT

One diagnosis can select the next behavior fix without repeating blind context runs.
