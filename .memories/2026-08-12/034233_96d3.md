## Restored family-store bridge parity without ordinary-read hydration

The first disk-backed context slice accidentally returned `bridge_requires_full_index` for family-store bridge traces.

- **What:** Added a context-local lazy bridge surface and a session-only `SessionBridgeGraphLoader`.
- **Why:** Family bridge output must remain byte-identical while context, impact, refs, and path avoid full repository hydration.
- **How:** The helper reuses `SqliteSymbolReader.ReadSession`, `SqliteBridgeReader.ReadSession`, the existing symbol-detail projection, provider selection, and `BridgeGraphBuilder`; `TraceTool` now consumes `ISymbolLookupIndex` plus `BridgeGraph` independently.
- **Impact:** Ordinary family resolve/path calls do zero bridge work; the first bridge call builds once within its pinned context; legacy and family bridge output match.
