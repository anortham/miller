## Exact four-pivot family reader is bounded

The rebuilt timeout appeared to stall before the first family-resolution completion, so the exact recovered four-id frontier was exercised once through the real pinned `IFamilyGraphResolutionReader` operation.

- **What:** Persisted EXPLAIN for all eight production arms, measured candidate hydration separately, and executed the actual reader once.
- **How:** Candidate hydration was 7.066 ms; all eight arms completed between 0.236 ms and 20.869 ms and merged 180 edges.
- **Why:** This tests the exact runtime candidate order without repeating a context call or guessing at a behavior fix.
- **Impact:** Rejects the family reader SQL arms as the multi-second bottleneck for this pinned-session shape; no production change is justified by this evidence.
