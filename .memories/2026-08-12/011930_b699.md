- Kept the producer boundary behind `MillerExtractContract`; no new module, migration, interface, or MCP tool was added.
- The live 2.31.4 Miller service entered `resolution=converging`; trace and later inspect calls returned `resolution_converging`, and refresh was refused because this session was not leader. Per the execution contract, stopped refresh/full attempts and used bounded file-level fallback only.
