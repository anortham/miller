## WHAT

Redesigned the FTS word arm to score a narrow projection and hydrate full symbol metadata only for ranked survivors.

## WHY

The candidate diagnosis measured 57,068 full candidate rows per context, costing 451 ms to read plus 266 ms to re-tokenize/score. A production-shaped RED proved a 7-result query hydrated all 1,200 matching symbols.

## HOW

`symbols_fts.body` is the writer’s documented exact space-joined CodeTokenizer stream with duplicates. The reader now selects only body, doc ID, name, kind, and doc length; computes the existing BM25/exact-name score for every candidate; preserves all word-matched IDs for trigram suppression; sorts identically; then hydrates only `min(wordHits, limit)` full rows in 500-ID chunks and restores ranked order. Added fixed WordHydration telemetry.

## IMPACT

The 7-result high-fanout test now hydrates exactly 7 rows with exact in-memory IDs, scores, and order. Public interfaces and search.db schema are unchanged.
