## WHAT

Implemented the PERF-002 lazy family-store generation path. `IndexHolder` now atomically publishes eager revision/artifact/count metadata with a single-flight lazy `MillerRepositoryIndex`. Family bootstrap and freshness install generation-pinned factories; current family read routes and workspace status do not evaluate them. Legacy read routes still take one atomic repository/revision snapshot.

## WHY

Every Miller host was eagerly hydrating the complete family-store repository during bootstrap and freshness, retaining roughly 1–2GB and contributing to 101.5GB of logical reads while idle. Disk-backed interactive reads had already removed the need for a resident full repository.

## HOW

Added `MetadataSnapshot` and `SwapLazy`, a lazy freshness decision path, bounded `WorkspaceIndexFactsReader` reads at bootstrap/refresh, metadata-only status, and mode-first provider routing. Deferred factories validate the captured full `IndexIdentity` before loading so a newer CURRENT generation cannot be paired with older metadata. Kept legacy mode eager and atomic.

## IMPACT

Current family context/search/inspect/artifact routes and workspace status remain disk-backed without full holder hydration. Explicit `Current`, legacy reads, and edit paths may materialize once. Affected ceiling passed 246/246 in 1 second test time. One attempted worktree registration independently reproduced runaway startup behavior (>90s, extractor ~115% CPU/1.8GB RSS) and was not retried.
