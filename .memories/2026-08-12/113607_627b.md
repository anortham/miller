## WHAT

Fused source-rescue phrase verification and best-line/snippet selection into one raw-text analysis pass per hydrated candidate.

## WHY

The accepted diagnostic measured 83 ms phrase verification plus 82 ms snippet selection over the same 116 chunks; both normalized, split, and tokenized every line independently.

## HOW

One local normalized-line array is tokenized once per line. The pass accumulates same-line phrase truth and applies the unchanged phrase-first, distinct coverage, token-hit, earliest-line rule, then slices the same snippet. Nothing is retained beyond the candidate call. Fixed telemetry now reports one honest RawTextAnalysis family.

## IMPACT

Production phrase fixture reports 81 hydrations/81 analyses with exact independent index score/path/line/snippet/order parity; cross-line phrase remains rejected.
