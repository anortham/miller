## WHAT

Closed two release-blocking semantic defects on `release/1.18.3`.

- Family-store convergence pauses now carry a scope token derived from every `StoreSidecarStamp` field; readers ignore missing or mismatched scopes.
- Convergence rewrites same-state pauses when the store scope changes and clears state, reason, and scope together.
- Shell and PowerShell soak readiness waits now fail immediately on a probe `failed` event; shell no longer ignores the two-model readiness result.

## WHY

Final review proved an old pause could mask a newer family-store snapshot. The first 30-minute release soak also wasted the entire long phase after the unprepared fallback model had already emitted a failed readiness event.

## HOW

- Luna used TDD for pause scoping: 76 passed / 6 intended failures RED, then 82/82 GREEN.
- The pause token uses null-safe length-delimited canonical material and SHA-256 across all 13 stamp fields.
- Soak phase order, success path, summary schema, probe implementation, pins, and public CLI/MCP output remain unchanged.
- Lead review used refreshed Miller symbol/impact evidence and exact interface references.

## IMPACT

Stale diagnostic metadata can no longer misdirect operators, and missing secondary models fail the soak promptly. The candidate is ready for a new exact-HEAD build, fast/scale/security gate, and full semantic soak with both pinned models prepared.
