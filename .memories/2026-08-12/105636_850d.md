## WHAT

Removed the per-candidate token-array/sub-string allocations from FTS word scoring.

## WHY

After narrow hydration, word scoring still consumed 147 ms across 57,068 candidates because `string.Split` allocated one array and token strings for every candidate.

## HOW

An ordinal `ReadOnlySpan<char>` scanner counts exact space-delimited occurrences directly in the writer-stored token body. It handles duplicates and defensive extra spaces while preserving exact boundaries and non-ASCII comparison.

## IMPACT

A warmed 10,000-call contract allocates exactly zero bytes. All FTS ranking, AND, signature, non-ASCII, hydration, and trigram parity remains green.
