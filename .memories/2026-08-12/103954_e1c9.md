## WHAT

Skipped the additive trigram arm when sorted word hits already fill the requested result limit.

## WHY

The FTS diagnostic measured trigram candidates at 159 ms / 1,800 rows. Trigram-only hits are always appended after every scored word hit and then final truncation applies, so they cannot affect output once word hits meet the limit.

## HOW

The arm now requires `wordHits.Count < limit`. High-fanout and exact-limit tests prove identical doc order and scores when skipped; a below-limit test proves interior-substring recall still executes and returns the trigram-only hit.

## IMPACT

Removes provably unused SQLite trigram reads without changing ranking or recall for any result that could be returned.
