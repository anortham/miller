## Moved family relationship graph reads below compatibility views

- **Why:** Family relationship reads only need pinned visible-version filtering; the compatibility projection imposed unnecessary query overhead.
- **How:** `FamilyStoreReadSession` runs fixed forward/reverse queries on `main.relationships`, validates visible source/endpoint versions, and returns storage-neutral edges to `SqliteSymbolGraphIndex`.
- **Impact:** Family mode executes zero compatibility relationship statements; legacy handles retain two relationship and two unresolved-name compatibility statements.
