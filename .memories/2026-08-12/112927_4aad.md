## WHAT

Moved exact content TF/BM25/coverage scoring before raw chunk hydration.

## WHY

The scoring diagnostic proved token/phrase work consumed 518 ms across 1,832 hydrated chunks. A high-fanout RED showed 601 raw chunks hydrated to return one result.

## HOW

Candidate SQL returns chunk ID plus the writer-guaranteed space-joined tokenizer body. Exact token frequency, BM25, and coverage run on that narrow body and cached doc length. Only score/coverage survivors are hydrated. Phrase verification and snippets remain post-hydration because the FTS body loses line boundaries. Strict/widened sequencing, kind/test filtering, seen IDs, caps, sorting, and symbol mapping remain unchanged.

## IMPACT

The high-fanout fixture now scores 601 narrow rows but hydrates and phrase-checks exactly one, with exact path, score, line, snippet, and containing-symbol parity.
