## Forwarded nullable family graph capabilities through WorkspaceReadHandle

Production graph construction wraps `FamilyStoreReadSession` in `WorkspaceReadHandle`, which hid both internal family graph capabilities and silently selected legacy compatibility SQL.

- **What:** Added nullable internal capability accessors on the handle and captured them independently in `SqliteSymbolGraphIndex`.
- **Why:** Direct-session tests passed, but the real provider shape never reached either the indexed resolution reader or unresolved-name reader.
- **How:** The handle continues to implement only public `IWorkspaceReadSession`; legacy handles expose null capabilities and retain legacy SQL.
- **Impact:** Production family graphs can now use both storage-owned optimized paths without widening any public contract.
