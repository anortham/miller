## WHAT

Reworked multi-term symbol FTS scoring to scan each stored token body once and avoid per-candidate frequency allocations.

## WHY

The candidate still spent ~242 ms scoring 57,068 rows; the main natural-language query rescanned each body once per nine distinct terms and allocated an `int[]` per candidate.

## HOW

Ordinal term hashes are computed once. Each candidate token stream is scanned once with collision-safe ordinal equality. The common measured shape uses a pooled flat frequency matrix capped at 1,000,000 cells (~4 MB), allowing exact corpus DF then scoring without rescanning. Oversized shapes use one reusable pooled term buffer and two candidate passes, bounding retained memory. Exact first-seen terms, duplicate TF, DF, AND matches, BM25, non-ASCII, and ordering remain unchanged.

## IMPACT

Nine-term high-fanout parity is exact, helper token traversal is one pass, and the helper allocates zero bytes.
