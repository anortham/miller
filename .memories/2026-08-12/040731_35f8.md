## Faithful Julie query replay identified two equal-sized bottlenecks

- **WHAT:** Reused a reflink clone of the prior clean 1,538-file performance fixture with its ready 392,526-identifier predecessor base. The single scoped diagnostic completed under the hard limit and emitted all fourteen query-family counters.
- **WHY:** Two synthetic hypotheses were disproven and a fresh fixture build itself exceeded 60 seconds. Reusing verified immutable evidence avoided another blind setup run and produced the production-scale access distribution.
- **HOW:** The clone was reset to a generation-2 converging view bound to the ready predecessor; source stayed exact and unchanged. Integrity/FK/hash/readiness checks passed before one diagnostic. LocateIdentifier executed 10,804 times—72.12% of 14,980 executions and exactly the pending count—inside materialized_relationship_covers' per-row loop. IdentifierHydration read 381,722 rows and PrimeWindow read 313,107. The resolver took 24.848s, while test process wall was 49.81s, exposing another ~24.96s in exact finalization/other work.
- **IMPACT:** Task 3 is now evidence-selected: eliminate or batch the per-row relationship coverage locator calls, then instrument/optimize exact finalization. No speculative cache or index-only fix is justified; both locator indexes already exist.
