## Added durable store family and view identity

- **WHAT:** Added registry migrations for `store_families` and `store_members`, a contained atomic workspace pointer, and store-catalog reconciliation.
- **WHY:** Miller needs stable family/view identities before it can route reads and writes to the julie-extract 2.31 family store.
- **HOW:** Git lineages share a minted UUID family; non-Git roots remain singleton families. Unknown lineage evidence can be promoted in place, while replacement requires an observed disappearance and a changed known root identity. The serving store catalog overrides stale registry and pointer caches. Symlink and malformed-catalog checks occur before registry mutation.
- **IMPACT:** Task 3 of the Ph3 wiring plan is complete and unblocks the read-session seam.
