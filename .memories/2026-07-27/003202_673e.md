## Made compact diagnostic attachment idempotent

- **WHAT:** `ToolDiagnosticRenderer.Attach` now adds compact diagnostic fields only when the payload does not already contain that field.
- **WHY:** Content's legacy compact failure payload already carried `diagnostic_code`; the shared typed diagnostic attachment appended it again, wasting tokens and making the contract ambiguous.
- **HOW:** Centralized line-prefix detection for `diagnostic_code` and `diagnostic_class`, with regression coverage at the renderer and public Content surface.
- **IMPACT:** Compact diagnostics across all nine tools have one canonical code/class field even when a payload was already partially typed.
