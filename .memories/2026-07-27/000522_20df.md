## Fixed zero-evidence identifier search false positives

- **WHAT:** Symbol search now treats underscores as logical term separators and refuses zero-evidence OR relaxation for identifier-like queries while preserving zero-evidence relaxation for natural-language queries.
- **WHY:** `NoSuchMillerSymbol_7f6e4d3c` returned unrelated `GetUserId`/fixture constants rather than an honest empty result.
- **HOW:** Shared strict-first relaxation counts underscore-separated terms; its admission decision receives an explicit natural-language allowance. Identifier queries with no strict population stay empty; natural-language recall behavior remains intact.
- **IMPACT:** Impossible identifier searches no longer spend tokens on high-scoring noise from alphanumeric fragments, without regressing conceptual multi-term recall.
