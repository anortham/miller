## Dogfood find: `semantic prepare` doesn't heal a live session — broker and circuit both hold `model_not_prepared` until restart

**WHAT:** On the fresh dev machine, `vectors: circuit-open (model_not_prepared)` persisted ≥12 minutes AFTER a successful `miller semantic prepare` (bge-small-en-v1.5-f32, sha-verified into `~/.cache/julie-semantic/`). Probed the julie.embedding.sidecar v1 `health` method directly at both ends:

- Fresh one-shot `julie-semantic-sidecar serve`: `ready:true`, model loaded, `resolved_backend:vulkan` (Intel UHD ADL-S GT1), dims 384.
- The RUNNING broker (same socket Miller's leader uses): `ready:false, degraded_reason:model_not_prepared`.

**WHY it sticks (two independent latches):**
1. **Sidecar side:** the broker stats the model cache once at spawn and never re-checks; `prepare` is a separate one-shot subprocess (the RPC protocol deliberately has no download method, design doc §), so the live broker never learns the model arrived.
2. **Miller side:** `SemanticEmbeddingSession` opens its circuit permanently for the process lifetime (`ModelNotPrepared_IsAStatedRefusalWithNoRestartLoop` — designed stated refusal), so even a broker fix wouldn't heal a running leader.

Recovery is a plain session restart: the broker dies with its owner (verified: previous session exit killed its broker; the new session respawned one), and a fresh broker stats the now-present model.

**Product gaps worth fixing:**
- `miller semantic prepare` ends at `{"event":"done"}` with no hint that a live Miller/broker won't activate until restart — and dashboard/status affordances are designed to shell out to this verb mid-session. It should at least print the restart instruction; better, nudge the broker.
- `workspace health` `recommended_actions` says "keep a resident Miller leader running so vectors can converge or rebuild" — actively wrong in this state; the leader can wait forever.
- `workspace status` compact shows only `vectors: circuit-open` with no reason; the `model_not_prepared` reason and the prepare verb only surface in health JSON.
- Broker re-stat-on-health-while-unready (cheap stat per health call) belongs in julie-extractors' sidecar, not Miller.

**Also observed (expected-by-design, not bugs):** first-build escalation loop logs `FullRebuildSignalled` then `BatchTooLarge` every 5-min tick while parked — correct vectors-v1 triggers, though the log reason names the trigger rather than the parked-on blocker.

**IMPACT:** This machine embeds Vulkan-accelerated on the iGPU once restarted. Fresh-install onboarding (every new Miller user who runs prepare mid-session) hits this exact wall.
