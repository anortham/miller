## Froze shared semantic broker contract v1

Concurrent Miller sessions need one reliable embedding model without recreating Julie's general daemon failure modes.

- **What:** Added the normative broker lifecycle, identity, local IPC, ownership, scheduling, accelerator, security, and lexical fail-open contract plus a contract guard.
- **Why:** Every later Miller and `julie-semantic-sidecar` implementation task needs one stable boundary before semantic retrieval can become default-on.
- **How:** Kept the broker pure compute over frozen `julie.embedding.sidecar` v1, used per-model service locks and one user-global accelerator lock, made stdin EOF authoritative, and explicitly superseded process-local lifecycle text in the ADR and historical designs.
- **Impact:** Unblocks implementation without adding an MCP tool, workspace ownership, HTTP/PID/state/token control planes, or weakening `MILLER_SEMANTIC=off`.
