- **How:** Added shared recorded-NVIDIA validation requiring `gpu.pass=true`, an accelerated warm model, exactly one warm broker, at least 64 MiB of warm growth, and many-session growth no more than warm plus 256 MiB. Acceptance and proof fields must agree for both passed and explicitly unavailable states.

- **GREEN:** Twelve validator cases pass. The focused validator plus Scale run passed 14 with two expected platform/runtime skips, and the recorded live summary verified through the CLI.
- **Impact:** CPU zeros, null/false GPU proof, and contradictory acceptance can no longer produce a false GPU pass.
