## Separated Miller factory recovery from sidecar service arbitration

Grok's progress review found two remaining phrases that conflated the Miller owner with the sidecar service-lock holder.

- **What:** Reworded broker recovery so Miller ownership is restored through factory lifecycle, while the model service lock selects only which sidecar service broker may load and serve.
- **Why:** A service lock cannot transfer the owner stdin/Job lease; leaving that implication would misdirect both the Miller factory and Rust broker implementations.
- **How:** Added a focused contract test first, observed it fail on the old phrases, then corrected the frozen contract and historical integration-design summary.
- **Impact:** Later lifecycle code now has one unambiguous ownership model: Miller factory owns lifetime; sidecar service broker owns service/accelerator locks.
