## WHAT

Refactored Miller semantic sessions around async transport-neutral connection and factory interfaces while preserving the current stdio child adapter.

## WHY

Task 7 must connect sessions to a shared broker without branching the session state machine on process versus IPC transport.

## HOW

- `SemanticEmbeddingSession` awaits factory connections, aborts only failed connections, and reconnects through the factory.
- Normal disposal closes the session connection without protocol shutdown.
- Borrowed factories remain alive; evaluation and package-smoke sessions explicitly own their stdio factories.
- A narrow compatibility adapter keeps current production and scale callers working until Task 7 rewires them.

## IMPACT

The shared-broker transport can be added behind the new seam without changing retry, handshake, circuit, timeout, or lexical fail-open policy.
