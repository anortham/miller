## Codex review fix 1 landed: admission retry re-validates the root at fire time

**WHAT:** `IndexBootstrapService.ScheduleAdmissionRetry` now calls a new `RetryRootStillBindable` guard after the delay, before `lock(_gate)`: re-runs `CanonicalizeAndRejectSensitiveRoot`, requires the directory to exist and the re-canonicalized path to `RootPathsEqual`-match the captured root; otherwise drops the retry with one Information line. Two new tests (vanished root, symlink-swapped root — real symlink, Windows skip).

**WHY:** Codex pre-merge review (verified real-improvement): the retry re-entered `RunBootstrapInBackground` with a root validated only at bind time — a delete or symlink swap during the ~60s delay could recreate a deleted path's `.miller` or scan a new occupant under the old identity, past the sensitive-root guard.

**HOW:** fix-f1 worker (restarted once after a connection-drop failure) under parallel-lead-commit; red→green with a re-proof after the test arrangement changed (`MarkBootstrapFailed` recreates `.miller` AFTER the failure, so the delete needed that barrier). Release build 0W/0E; fast suite 6148/0 (tripwire 42s under fleet2 load — re-judge at final gate).

**IMPACT:** Both codex findings fixed. Worker-flagged follow-up (out of scope): `MarkBootstrapFailed` itself recreates `<root>/.miller` for an already-vanished root; the registry row + presence monitor own that story.
