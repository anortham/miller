**WHY:** The test pinned the T1-removed behavior. It held the target's `content.lock` so a spawned `workspace open --full` parked at sidecar convergence WHILE holding machine-wide scan admission. - Product code untouched: the test pinned removed behavior; T1's release-after-scan is the approved design.
