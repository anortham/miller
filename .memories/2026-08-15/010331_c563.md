## WHAT

Confirmed both remaining Task 8 performance root causes and selected the minimum-contract architecture direction.

## WHY

The strict replay showed a common 6.8-second read floor and a 60-second one-file producer timeout. Fixes require measured ownership and exact public-path classification.

## HOW

- Method-level probes across four inspect and four impact runs attribute 6.34–6.37 seconds (93–94% of total CLI time) to `RepositoryIndexLoader.LoadSession`.
- Identifier/pending relationship reads, index build, and symbol hydration account for 5.74–5.75 seconds (84.6–84.8% of the full CLI floor).
- `FamilyStoreReadSession.Open` is only 170.77 ms and compatibility projection is 3.54 ms, closing the earlier temp-table hypothesis.
- Architecture lanes converged on existing revision-pinned SQLite/search data plus `SqliteSymbolGraphIndex(session)`, with full in-memory hydration lazy and limited to bridge trace or any operation proven to need it.
- A fresh public one-file observation completed in 151.462 seconds, exit 0, mode `Full/Crossover`, fallback `resolution_scope_crossover`; resolution alone took 146.468 seconds. Exact parity passed and peak PSS was 25.23 MiB.
- The producer fix must change the crossover policy/work estimate while preserving exact resolution parity; raising the timeout is not a fix.

## IMPACT

The implementation can now target two bounded structural causes rather than database tuning in general. The Miller fix should remove about 5.75 seconds from common one-shot reads without new storage. The Julie fix should keep a one-file request scoped when scoped resolution is cheaper than the current coarse 70% promotion rule.
