## WHAT

Completed Task 9's identical production-volume after replay and recorded the result in the recovery plan and progress ledger.

## WHY

The bounded exact-key child-name batch could only be retained if a fresh copied-store replay proved a material improvement without weakening full/exact behavior.

## HOW

The exact 1,502-file, 402,195-identifier workload ran through the public `producer.resolve.full` path against a disposable store copy. Wall fell from 148,431 ms to 128,397 ms, CPU from 148,034 ms to 114,673 ms, and the resolution phase from 139,497 ms to 118,402 ms. `children_named` fell from 79,508 ms to 40,838 ms and from 715,409 to 305,632 executions. The run exited 0, did not time out, remained full/exact with matching exact-at identity, used 27,015,168-byte peak PSS, and restored the staged pointer to its required SHA-256.

## IMPACT

Task 9 is a validated 13.5% end-to-end / 48.6% query-family improvement and is retained. The 60-second Linux gate remains open. `children_named` is still the largest family, and `symbol_by_id` regressed from 224,645 executions/3,399 ms to 466,860/6,465 ms, so the next change must explain remaining scalar calls and bounded-cache displacement before implementation.
