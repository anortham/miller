## WHAT

Added Task 9 to the performance-recovery plan with the approved architecture and TDD acceptance gates for the measured `children_named` N+1.

## WHY

The exact copied-store profile proves this single query family owns 79.508 seconds and 715,409 executions. The next change must remove statement amplification without weakening resolver correctness or creating an unbounded cache.

## HOW

The store session will derive at most one window of exact `(version_id, parent_symbol_id, name)` keys, use bounded count/fetch statements against the existing parent index, cache only complete per-key results inside the existing phase window, and preserve the scalar fallback. Query-count, ordering/early-stop, memory/page, and full/scoped digest tests are caller-facing.

## IMPACT

Diagnosis is complete and the serialized TDD implementation slice is fully specified. No public API, Store Contract, schema, resolver-tier, or feature change is permitted. The identical production-volume replay remains the after-number gate.
