## Architecture selected for the remaining producer bottlenecks

### WHAT

Selected two serialized internal changes after aggregate profiling and parallel architecture review.

### WHY

The scoped one-file path cannot meet 5 s by fixing only one owner: coverage amplification costs 4.230 s and full exact finalization costs 5.562 s inside an 11.508 s resolution phase.

### HOW

1. Extend the internal `ResolutionSession` seam with a bounded batch coverage operation. The Store adapter owns set-based SQL and overlay semantics; the scalar method remains the compatibility/default implementation.
2. Introduce one internal common-caller finalization result. Scoped execution writes the existing delta scratch directly from frozen prior state plus touched rows. Forced-full execution retains `finish_exact` plus `stream_resolution_diff` as the exact oracle. Both keep the current fenced/CAS publisher.

Tests are caller-facing and deterministic: O(chunks) reader/query work, scratch/phase invalidation, pending-first overlay precedence, locator/manifest predicates, totality/target rejection, atomic publication, exact rows/order/digest, forced-full parity, and real cross-language extracts.

### IMPACT

The design removes both measured structural costs without a schema/public-contract change, unbounded cache, relationship simplification, or Windows-specific fork. Implementation can proceed as two isolated TDD slices with a focused replay between them.
