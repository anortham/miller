## WHAT

Completed Task 11A's partitioned candidate-cache implementation, lead-review correction, focused verification, and identical production-volume replay.

## WHY

Task 10 proved Task 9 bulk promotion overfilled and displaced the by-ID cache. The partition had to prove a real net latency win, not only a cleaner bound.

## HOW

A caller-facing RED reproduced max by-ID occupancy 301 for W=300. The store adapter now reserves W by-ID logical entries for explicit SymbolById lookups and caps existing non-by-ID entries at 2W; exact/name caches no longer bulk-promote hits. Exact-key admission charges 1+raw_count. Focused Task 9/10 parity/count/order/attribution tests, all four cache bounds, format, check, and strict clippy pass. The exact disposable replay completed full/exact with exit 0 and restored the staged pointer.

## IMPACT

Full wall improved 129,402 -> 120,697 ms and resolution 119,376 -> 110,591 ms. SymbolById fell 466,860 -> 91,259 SQL executions and 6,462 -> 1,648 ms; cache hits rose to 668,817, rejections fell to zero, and max by-ID/non-by-ID/total occupancy is 144/600/742. Child SQL also fell 305,632 -> 282,489 and 40,846 -> 37,604 ms. Scope chains still own 235,307/279,272 scalar pages (84.26%), so completed scalar-key reuse is the next measured slice.
