## WHAT

Recovered the exact crossover operands for the public README-only change and approved a narrow all-language producer fix.

## WHY

The public one-file request selected Full/Crossover and spent 146.468 seconds resolving. The policy could not be changed safely until its pre-promotion counts and resolver compatibility rules were proven.

## HOW

- Scope-only diagnostic recovered 1 changed file, 39 names, 775 selected versions, 384,648 scoped identifiers, and 432,203 total identifiers; the 70% threshold is 302,542.1.
- Raw `versions_matching_names` pulled 774 collision versions: 754 C# and 20 Razor, carrying 382,458 identifiers.
- The changed README version is Markdown, contains zero identifiers/pending rows, and contributes only the directly changed version.
- Every resolver candidate tier already requires candidate language to equal edge language. The cross-language C#/Razor expansion can never produce a valid target.
- Approved design: derive affected languages from both old and new journal versions, fail closed if unavailable, and constrain only name-to-version expansion to those languages. Keep the name arm, 0.7 threshold, same-language broad-collision crossover, store contract, and public interfaces unchanged.

## IMPACT

The captured corpus prediction falls from 384,648 scoped identifiers to the unchanged 2,190-name arm, allowing bounded scoped resolution. Same-language broad collisions still cross over, and all supported languages use the same generic predicate.
