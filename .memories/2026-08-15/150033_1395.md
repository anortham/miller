## WHAT

Added Task 11A and a bounded Luna implementation brief for a protected candidate-window by-ID budget.

## WHY

Task 10 proves Task 9's exact/name bulk promotion can grow the by-ID map to 378 entries for a 300 budget and causes 438,374 by-ID-cap plus 289,068 overlapping aggregate-cap rejections. That measured regression should be corrected independently before scope-key reuse.

## HOW

The existing 3W logical capacity becomes two private partitions: by-ID <= W and current non-by-ID logical entries <= 2W. Only explicit `symbol_by_id` creates by-ID keys; complete name and exact-child caches keep their own results without promotion. Exact-child batch selection charges 1 + raw_count rather than 1 + 2*raw_count while preserving raw-row, ordering, visibility, and page bounds. Cloned values remain to keep the A/B local.

## IMPACT

Task 11A should restore useful direct symbol caching and may admit more exact-child keys without increasing memory. One identical replay will decide its value before the separately designed complete scalar-key reuse slice.
