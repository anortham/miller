## WHAT

Added Task 11B and its TDD execution brief for completed scalar child-key reuse.

## WHY

After Task 11A, dynamic tier-3 receiver and tier-1 terminal scope walks still own 235,307 scalar pages, or 84.26% of remaining child SQL. These keys are discovered during resolver traversal and cannot all be safely prefetched from the phase chunk.

## HOW

The store adapter buffers known hits only within current non-by-ID headroom and at most one window. It admits the existing exact key only after the raw cursor reaches terminal exhaustion. Early stop, callback/SQL error, overflow, or incomplete pagination never cache. Empty and unknown-kind-filtered complete results are valid. All Task 11A bounds and phase reset remain unchanged.

## IMPACT

This is the smallest local change that can remove repeated ancestor/scope SQL without speculative reads or a new interface. Caller-facing RED/GREEN and one exact replay will determine whether it earns retention.
