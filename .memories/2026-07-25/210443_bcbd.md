## Completed the Content takeover remediation

### WHAT

Reworked Miller Content into a revision-safe, failure-isolated, byte-bounded agent surface and replaced the superseded v2 contract with `content-mcp-v3.md`.

### WHY

The first Phase 7 slice bounded list/shape but fresh Claude review found stale reads, fail-fast cross-workspace behavior, misleading counts/diagnostics, broad raw-text allocation, source-drift blindness, and several remaining paths that could flood agent context.

### HOW

- Enforced a universal 12 KiB MCP response ceiling and bounded caller-controlled inputs.
- Added unified schema-v3 search/read output, probe-truthful counts, explicit all-kind search, fair local-rank workspace fusion, and bounded degradation details.
- Failed closed on stale or missing workspace indexes while preserving healthy partial results with `workspace_search_incomplete`.
- Loaded FTS raw text only for at most 5,000 ranked candidates in 400-row batches.
- Carried `content_hash` through import/search/read/shape/list and made store metadata/chunk reads transactional.
- Added truthful clamp/omission fields and contiguous multi-line continuation.
- Preserved CLI limits/list behavior, streamed JSONL directly with literal LF, and removed MCP export/v2 rather than deprecating them.
- Ran iterative Claude reviews until the final targeted verdict was clean.

### IMPACT

Content remains a decisive Miller advantage over Julie's ephemeral spillover workflow, but now delivers bounded, current, actionable evidence without false absence claims or whole-corpus allocations.
