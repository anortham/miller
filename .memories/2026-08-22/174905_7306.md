## WHAT

Hardened the Unix CT daemon launcher so Linux daemons survive full launcher process-group teardown. Added a behavior-level fallback test for Unix systems without `setsid` and a Linux Scale test proving the daemon moves to a distinct session and process group while the published PID remains the live lease PID.

## WHY

`exec nohup` fixed the wrapper-PID race but did not detach the daemon from the launching session. Live `tests serve --json` published `ready`, then the daemon was killed as soon as the CLI invocation ended, leaving stale lease/status files.

## HOW

The shell script detects `setsid` through `PATH` and runs `exec setsid nohup ...` when available; otherwise it retains `exec nohup ...` for supported Unix platforms such as macOS where `setsid` may be absent. The Linux regression kills the launcher process group and checks session separation plus PID identity.

## IMPACT

Live Miller status now remains `running` after `tests serve` exits, with the daemon PID equal to its own session and process-group IDs. This unblocks real daemon-path CT replay and preserves non-Linux Unix compatibility.
