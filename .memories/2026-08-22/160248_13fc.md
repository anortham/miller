## Task 5A1 — daemon publication readiness

### WHAT

Added bounded publication readiness to `CtDaemonLauncher.SpawnDetached`. Successful starts now carry `ready`, `not_published_within_grace`, or `daemon_exited_before_publish` plus elapsed time; process-launch status and reason remain unchanged.

### WHY

A detached process can be accepted by the OS before its lease/status files appear. Treating that publication lag as a dead daemon is misleading, while an actual early process exit must remain distinguishable.

### HOW

Added injectable lease/status readers, process-liveness probe, `TimeProvider`, grace, poll interval, and delay seams. Readiness requires a live lease and matching non-stopped status; tests use a manual clock and condition-based bounded polling, so they never wait two wall-clock seconds.

### IMPACT

The launcher now exposes honest internal start truth for the next Task 5 rendering packet. Existing spawn statuses, reasons, root safety, version replacement, and shadow-copy behavior remain intact.
