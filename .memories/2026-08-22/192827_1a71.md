## WHAT

Completed sidecar Tasks 2 and 3. `IndexerService` now retries stale/failed content/search sidecars on quiet idle ticks with target-keyed 5s/15s/30s backoff, immediate reset for any changed target, success clearing, writer-lease enforcement, and store-off zero work. `VectorConvergeService` now waits for bootstrap binding, probes the durable vector completeness stamp before its first wait, suppresses drains/retries when exact, wakes after shadow promotion, and preserves semantic-off zero work.

## WHY

An unchanged workspace could remain stale indefinitely, and a resident vector process could sleep forever when the durable target lacked an exact completeness stamp. Retrying without gates risked extraction loops, cross-process contention, or semantic work while disabled.

## HOW

Retry state lives in singleton `IndexerService`, keyed by family/view/store instance/generation/sequence and driven by its injected clock. The production path requires both store mode and the live writer lease. Vector desired state is read from the current family-store sequence plus `StoreSidecarStamp`; bootstrap uses `WaitUntilBoundAsync`, and held retries re-probe exactness before waking.

## IMPACT

Quiet leaders self-heal derived sidecars without source changes or extraction. Current vector restarts do no embedding/cursor/retry work, shadow promotion cannot strand the chunk lane, and `MILLER_SEMANTIC=off` plus `MILLER_INDEX_STORE=off` remain zero-work for their respective paths.
