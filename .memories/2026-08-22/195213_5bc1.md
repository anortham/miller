## WHAT

Completed sidecar Task 4. Refresh results now carry an optional bounded `sidecars` object with exact target sequence, aggregate flags, and content/search/vector outcomes. CLI and MCP compact/JSON render it only when present. Health guidance now uses durable search/content/vector facts and `IsLeader` to distinguish queued resident work, missing leader, bounded failure retry, and model preparation.

## WHY

A successful unchanged refresh could hide repaired or failed sidecars, and missing vector stamps could lead users back to a one-shot refresh that cannot generate embeddings. The first implementation also placed carrier types in Tools and injected request-local outcomes into health with no production caller.

## HOW

Moved `SidecarConvergenceFacts` to the Workspaces boundary, propagated it through `WorkspaceRefreshResult`, and preserved schema-1 legacy bytes when absent. Health remains request-independent: it ranks durable vector facts based on resident leader ownership and gives bounded diagnostic/retry guidance for unreadable search/content state.

## IMPACT

Refresh output is truthful and bounded; health actions now name work that can actually progress. No new tool/schema was added, `vectors-v1.md` remains frozen, and old optional-field fixtures remain byte-identical.
