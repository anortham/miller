### The reviews caught two things worth remembering

1. **The seed-gate retrieval limit is a RANKING INPUT, not a perf knob** (HIGH). Sharing one retrieval at the larger limit widened the lexicalIds membership set, so RerankOnly admission accepted semantic seeds it used to drop — different bundles. Restored as named constant `SemanticSeedGateLimit = 2` with a doc comment; pinned by a fixture that self-proves it discriminates (mutation: widening the gate fails exactly 2 tests).
2. **The "can only fail open" existence-gate claim was false**: a JSON-escaped key spelling defeats a raw-text LIKE (parser matches unescaped names). Fixed as a true superset: LIKE prefilter + a backslash-triggered json_type() arm; measured zero cost on real stores (0 of 32,594 blobs carry a backslash).

Also: the vacuous A/B tests (passed with the change reverted, exercised a hand-copy) were deleted and replaced with a real end-to-end admission test — the "green run must prove the change" lesson again.
