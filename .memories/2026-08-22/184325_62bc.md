## WHAT

Implemented Task 1's typed `StoreSidecarConvergenceResult` boundary for content, search, vector, and metric work. Results carry exact target sequence, per-sidecar status/reason, did-work, pending, leader-required, aggregate failure, and bounded warning text. Resident and cross-workspace callers now consume the result.

## WHY

The prior void boundary logged convergence failures but let refresh appear clean. A one-shot process could stamp a process-local vector signal even though no resident loop could drain it.

## HOW

Content/search convergence returns current, repaired, disabled, or failed independently. Lease lifecycle errors mark all enabled affected sidecars failed. Vector work reports leader_required without a resident drain and queued with one; repeated targets remain queued until a later task proves exact completeness. The store sequence is resolved before every sidecar phase.

## IMPACT

Unchanged refreshes now have truthful internal evidence for Task 4 rendering, and Tasks 2-3 can consume one shared result boundary without new tools, schemas, or coordinators.
