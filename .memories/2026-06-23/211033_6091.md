## Added references JSONL export for Eros workflows

- **WHAT:** Added a modest `miller references export --jsonl` CLI/export surface backed by existing `identifiers` rows, plus capabilities advertisement and contract docs.
- **WHY:** Eros requested a bulk references/usage feed for dead-code workflows without moving ranking, suppressions, history, or dashboard workflow state into Miller.
- **HOW:** Implemented `ReferenceExportReader` in `Miller.Indexing`, wired it through `CliDispatch.ArtifactExport`, advertised it in `CliCapabilities`, and documented `references-export-v1` as an unresolved/name-based fact feed with nullable target fields.
- **IMPACT:** Miller now exposes deterministic usage facts for Eros ingestion while preserving the Miller/Eros product boundary.
