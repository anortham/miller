## WHAT

Corrected the two Task 7 review findings. Global `VSTest` evidence now runs the bounded MSBuild property probe, allowing inherited/evaluated MTP properties to produce a fail-closed conflict. The unused undocumented `ParseMachineResult` JSON-line parser and its helpers are removed.

## WHY

A non-empty global runner had been treated as fully decisive, so global VSTest could hide conflicting evaluated MTP selection. The machine-result parser had no callers or official contract and should not remain as dead behavior.

## HOW

- `ResolveBackendAsync` probes when the global runner is MTP or VSTest, while preserving the no-probe fast path for decisive xUnit v3 with no global runner.
- A provider-level regression asserts global VSTest plus evaluated `UseMicrosoftTestingPlatformRunner=true` probes first and refuses the conflict.
- Miller `inspect`, `trace`, and source search confirmed the removed parser had zero callers and no remaining source occurrence.
- Existing direct `dotnet exec` MTP argument routing and TRX-only result path are unchanged.

## IMPACT

Runner precedence is now fail-closed across global VSTest plus inherited MTP properties, with no redundant xUnit v3 probe. The MTP adapter has no invented machine-result fallback.
