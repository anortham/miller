## WHAT

Completed the lightweight edit-read slice. Store-mode edit preview/apply now runs against the pinned `IWorkspaceSymbolReadProvider` lookup instead of hydrating the full `MillerRepositoryIndex`; stale-span recovery reopens a fresh workspace symbol-read context. Legacy mode continues to reuse its eager snapshot.

## WHY

Telemetry showed edit preview calls paying repository-hydration cost, and stale retries remained bound to the old store session. Both caused avoidable latency and misleading recovery behavior.

## HOW

`EditTool` opens a `WorkspaceSymbolReadContext` and passes its lookup/session into `EditService`. `EditService` depends on `ISymbolLookupIndex` and uses a fresh-context resolver only for stale recovery. Tests cover all seven operations, fresh store-session recovery, store zero-materialization through the real provider, and legacy eager-index reuse.

## IMPACT

Edit operations no longer require full repository materialization in store mode, while preview/apply semantics and legacy behavior remain intact.
