## Root-caused telemetry performance and health anomalies

### WHAT

Completed read-only source tracing and current-build measurement for cold edit previews, context/impact tails, and the misleading health error warning.

### WHY

Release averages mixed historical defects, cold process loads, lagging sidecars, and graph-heavy workloads. Each symptom needed a separate measured cause before choosing fixes.

### HOW

- Edit preview: current 1.25 first `replace_text` preview took 5,164 ms; three warm repeats took 16 to 21 ms. The first edit materializes the full repository through `IndexHolder.Current` and `RepositoryIndexLoader.LoadSession`, including 143,134 symbols and 795,565 references. Freshness swaps reinstall the lazy loader.
- Context: three current 1.25 warm calls still took 13.6 to 18.5 seconds and issued 2,706 to 3,122 lookups. Full-query plus per-term retrieval and per-hit hydration create repeated SQLite work.
- Extreme context/impact tails share a lagging-sidecar defect: `SqliteSymbolReader.ReadForPaths` orders the whole symbol table in a CTE before filtering requested paths. Lookup consumed 94 to 97 percent of 40 to 65 second context calls.
- Normal impact cost scales with graph reach at about 10 ms per reached node. Current target-impact probes are under one second, but no comparable 1.25 changed-path/git-diff cohort exists.
- Health: `SummarizeOutcomes` counts every retained row while `WorkspaceHealthFacts.Create` calls the result recent. Status correctly uses the existing seven-day window.

### IMPACT

Priority order: fix lagging-sidecar path batching/ordering first because it affects context and impact; batch context lookup/hydration next; make health use the seven-day outcome window; then measure impact graph batches and decide whether edit should prewarm or use a narrower index.
