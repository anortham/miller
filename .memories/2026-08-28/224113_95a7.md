## WHAT

Implemented the producer view-retirement adapter around pinned `julie-extract store maintain retire-view`. It supports explicit preview/apply, validates report identity and counts, and returns planned, retired, already-absent, or failed outcomes.

## WHY

Workspace removal currently deletes registry membership and sidecars but leaves producer views and manifests alive. Orchestration needs one exact, bounded producer adapter before it can delete membership safely.

## HOW

The adapter consumes the captured `StoreSidecarReclaimTarget`, passes exact store/family/view arguments, validates schema/action/mode/family/view/disposition and all three retirement counts, and normalizes exact `view_not_found` to AlreadyAbsent. Process, timeout, malformed, mismatch, and count errors return Failed without throwing. Explicit dispositions prevent preview from being treated as completed retirement.

## IMPACT

Task 2 can now retire the producer view before registry deletion without learning extractor argv or JSON details. Report parsing runs cross-platform; only fake executable process tests are POSIX-specific.
