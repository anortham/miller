Completed the lagging-sidecar path-read correction. `ReadForPaths` now filters requested paths before window ranking, then returns deterministic contiguous document identities. Live rows replacing sidecar rows retain the sidecar `DocId`.

## WHY

Filtering after `ROW_NUMBER` ranked the entire workspace for a small changed-path set, and returning the live row's local `DocId` broke the stable identity used by search merge and later batched resolution.

## HOW

The path predicate moved into the ordered CTE, results are deterministically sorted and re-numbered after the existing 500-path batches, and the live wrapper copies the sidecar identity. Real SQLite tests prove 501 unique paths execute exactly two path statements despite duplicates. A real wrapper test captured the old semantic failure and verifies identity across 501 rows.

## IMPACT

Changed-path reads avoid full-table ranking, stable identities survive lagging-sidecar replacement, and Task 4 can safely add batched context hydration on top of these identities.
