## Routed full inspect through symbol lookup projection

Full `inspect` for a unique symbol no longer needs `RepositoryIndexLoader.Load` just to render child symbols.

- **WHAT:** Added `ISymbolLookupIndex.FindChildren`, backed by `SymbolLookupTables` and `FtsSymbolSearchIndex`, then routed MCP and CLI full `inspect` through the lookup projection for children/refs/callers/callees/body.
- **WHY:** TODO #5 still had unique-symbol full `inspect` on the graph-heavy path after the ambiguous-target preflight fix. On large workspaces this made one-shot CLI full inspect pay the full graph load cost unnecessarily.
- **HOW:** `InspectTool.RunLookup` now handles `depth=full` over any lookup backend. Full inspect uses `FindChildren` plus existing direct SQLite readers (`ReadDetail`, `ReadReferences`, `ReadCallees`, `ReadBody`) instead of the full repository projection.
- **IMPACT:** OpenClaw scoped unique full inspect (`runWorkspaceStatusHealth`) renders children/callees/body at `real=0.38s` with ~68 MB max RSS. TODO and dogfood findings now mark full `inspect` closed; remaining TODO #5 graph-heavy surfaces are `context`, `impact`, and `trace`.
