## Release boundaries

- The family store is default-on when `MILLER_INDEX_STORE` is unset or blank. Explicit
  `0|false|off|disabled` retains the existing export-before-serving compatibility path.
- A7 durable coordinator reader pins/GC lock ordering and A8 cursor-incremental search/content sidecars remain
  disclosed follow-up work. v1.18 does not claim them closed.
- Per-view vector sidecars remain the shipped design. Family-shared vectors require a separate visibility and
  cost proof.
- The release changes the default policy and producer pin; it does not redesign the family, view,
  coordinator, migration, rollback, or sidecar boundaries.
